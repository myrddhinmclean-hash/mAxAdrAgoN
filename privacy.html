Privacy

This site

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising network, no cookies set by me. The site is static files. Nothing here is watching you read.

Buying something

Payment is handled by the store the buy button sends you to. They collect what they need to take payment and they hold it under their own privacy policy, not mine. I never see your card details. What reaches me is that a sale happened, and an email address if you contact me.

Email

If you email me, I have your email. I use it to answer you. It does not go on a list, because there is no list.

The software

The tools sold here run on your machine. Where a tool does not need the internet, it does not use it. Anything that does talk to a network says so on its product page.
