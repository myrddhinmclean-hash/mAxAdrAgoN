# The Road to Paradiso, the Jail, and the Escape Glori Arranges

**Level:** 3
**Setting:** The granite toll road from the caverns to Paradiso; the Paradiso gates; the high-security imperial jail; the customs house; the docks.
**Main Threat:** The Empire occupation — bandit ambush on the road, border patrol at the gates, and the jail itself.
**Adventure Summary:** The party walks the road to Paradiso, is ambushed by bandits, is arrested at the gates by an Empire border patrol, watches Thorek the wagoneer executed at sundown in the market square, and is broken out of the imperial jail by an escape Glori arranges. The session ends with the party retrieving gear, the Multipass, and W3 from the customs house, and boarding the Empire transport *Tide of Froscius* at dawn of Day 10.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The road to Paradiso is a hard dirt track cut into the side of a granite hill, and the hill is the color of old iron, and the iron is what the Empire is mining. The party walks single file because the track is only wide enough for single file, and Thorek the wagoneer is behind with the covered wagon, wheels loud on the granite. Behind everything, far out on the western horizon, a heavy warship is visible against the cloud line — gaining, patient, not yet close enough to matter today. A bird of prey circles above the track. It has been circling since dawn.

* **Active Obstacle (Scheduled Event):** Four bandits hidden in the granite cuts, 30 ft. up, shortbows, signal horn trigger. The party has 3 rounds (about 6 real minutes) to spot them, take cover, or talk them down. At level 3 the first volley is 4 attacks at +4 for 1d6+2 each.
* **Tactical Objective:** Beat the horn. If it sounds: 2 rounds of volley fire, then the bandits close. Failure trigger: first volley at point-blank on the open track.
* **Transition Trigger:** An arrow hits the dirt at the party's feet. The bandit leader — Druda, a half-orc — shouts a demand for the wagon.

## 2. Universal Hooks

1. **The Wagoneer's Last Map.** Thorek carries a charcoal map of the road from Paradiso to Braaken on sailcloth, marking the Empire customs checkpoints. This is the party's guide at the docks.
2. **The Bandit Prisoner.** A halfling merchant tied at the bandit camp, tradeable for the wagon.
3. **The Empire Customs House.** Holds confiscated gear, the Multipass, and W3 in a locked strongbox on the customs officer's desk. The strongbox is a Session 10 thread.
4. **The Gates.** The border patrol — 4 humans in heavy armor, 6 kobolds — arrests anyone without valid Empire papers. The Multipass is the legitimate way through.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **Road Origin.** The road is a third-age dwarven granite toll road, Empire-paved in the second occupation year. **DC 10 History** or ask Thorek.
2. **Undercut Stretch.** A 30 ft. stretch undercut by winter flood collapses under the wagon. Cross on foot, rope-haul the wagon. **DC 10 Perception.**
3. **Druda's Cowardice.** Druda accepts 30 gp or the wagon's contents to let the party pass; he fears the border patrol. **DC 13 Insight.**
4. **The Deserters.** Two of four bandits are Empire deserters with garrison tattoos. **DC 13 Persuasion** flips them; the encounter drops to 0 XP and the party gains two road allies.
5. **The Drainage Channel.** A channel runs under the cell block floor to the docks. It is the escape's third step. **DC 13 Investigation** in the cell, or Glori reveals it.
6. **Thorek's Sentence.** The patrol will execute Thorek at sundown in the market square for guiding rebellion couriers. The party is in jail and cannot intervene. **DC 10 Insight** at arrest, or overhear the guards.
7. **The Puzzle Lock.** The cell lock is a dwarven three-ring puzzle lock; the combination is the current dwarven calendar date. Glori knows it. **DC 13 Investigation.**
8. **Verra the Commander.** The Paradiso garrison commander Verra is Empire regular, not EoE, and has been told the party is low priority — so no pursuit beyond Paradiso. **DC 10 History** in the jail.
9. **The Cookfire Stone.** Under the bandit cookfire: 20 gp, a patrol rotation list, and a sealed letter from Ulna to Thorek asking when the next courier flight is. **DC 13 Perception** or a deserter shows the party.
10. **The Tide of Froscius.** The ship at the docks is an Empire mail transport bound for Braaken, 12 marines, Cpt. Vrell commanding — regular navy, not EoE. **DC 13 Investigation** at the docks.

## 4. The Execution (Thorek's Single Death)

At sundown, from the jail's barred window, the party sees the Paradiso market square. Thorek is put against the customs house wall. A patrol officer reads the charge: guiding rebellion couriers. Thorek does not look at the officer. He looks at the second-story window of the jail, and he finds the party's faces, and he nods once, the way a man nods when the road he was on was the right road and it simply ended here. The volley is four muskets. The scene is public, brief, and the crowd is silent. Thorek dies exactly once, here, and nowhere else in Wave 1.

## 5. The Escape (Centerpiece & Playable Mechanics)

**The arranger:** Glori (Glorinath d'Braaken), elven prince of Braaken, is in the adjacent cell under a false name, and reveals himself only when the escape begins. He engineers a three-step escape: the shift-change handover, the drainage stealth, and the customs house recovery.

### STEP 1 — The Shift-Change Handover (Playable Mechanics)

* **Timing:** The execution occurs at sundown; moonset occurs approximately five hours later at midnight. This provides five in-game hours for the party to observe guard routines and prepare.
* **Watch Rotation & Guard Counts:** The daytime guard detail (4 veteran human guards) rotates off duty at midnight. The incoming night watch consists of only 2 green conscripts who have never seen the prisoners' faces.
* **Handover Location & Blind Spot:** The guard exchange takes place in the outer guardhouse around the corner from the cell block, leaving the main corridor completely unattended.
* **Vulnerable Window:** The handover lasts exactly 10 minutes. During this window, no sentry is posted inside the cell block.
* **Scouting & Observation:** Glori alerts the party to the shift-change routine (**DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** to confirm the guardhouse footsteps).
* **The Puzzle Lock:** The cell door is secured with a dwarven three-ring puzzle lock set to the current dwarven calendar date. Turning the rings from the inside requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** or **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check using a small wire or iron nail provided by Glori. Success opens the cell silently; failure on by 5 or more alerts the outer conscripts as they return.
* **Stealth Exit:** Once unlocked, the party slips through the corridor during the ten-minute handover window on a group **DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** check.

### STEP 2 — The Drainage Channel
Out through the drainage channel under the cell block floor (Secret 5), requiring a **DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth)** group check. Failure cost: a sudden encounter with 2 patrolling kobolds in the alley (no general alarm raised on a partial success).

### STEP 3 — The Customs House Recovery
The strongbox in the customs office holds confiscated gear, the Multipass, and W3. The box is secured with the same dwarven date-lock as the cell (Secret 7), opened via **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** or entering the date code. W3 is in the party's possession from this scene forward (canary note for downstream modules).

**Closing beat:** At dawn of Day 10 the party boards the *Tide of Froscius* using the Multipass and the forged writ of transit. The warship on the western horizon is now close enough to matter as the sea crossing begins.
