# Garegstown and the Scorched Withdrawal

**Level:** 2
**Setting:** Garegstown, the cliffside docks, the warehouse district, the market, and the Empire command post. The Empire is burning what it cannot carry.
**Main Threat:** The Empire occupation in its withdrawal form, in the person of Kobold Captain Skritch and the Empire garrison detail overseeing the scorched earth
**Adventure Summary:** The party is in Garegstown on the morning after the cavern escape, and the Empire garrison has begun burning the warehouses, the ships, and the supplies it cannot carry. If the party does not intervene, the Garegstown civilians lose their winter food stores and the rebellion loses its foothold in the port. If the party does intervene, the Empire command post becomes a fight and Skritch becomes the session's named antagonist.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The Garegstown warehouse district is on fire, and the fire has been on fire for an hour, and the fire is not an accident. Empire soldiers are walking the docks with torches, and the torches are being put to warehouse doors, and the warehouse doors are old wood, and the old wood is taking the torches the way old wood does, which is fast and loud. The smoke is the color of a thunderhead, and the thunderhead is rolling down the cliff face toward the market, and the market is where the civilians are, and the civilians are not running yet.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** Empire soldiers are burning the warehouses. The party has 3 rounds of in game time (about 6 real world minutes) to choose: stop the burning, evacuate the civilians, or let the Empire withdraw. The Empire soldiers are 4 humans in heavy armor plus 4 kobolds, working in pairs.
* **Tactical Objective:** Save at least one warehouse from the burning, or evacuate the market, or both. The party has limited time and limited resources. The warehouses contain the Garegstown winter food stores. Failure trigger: the warehouses burn and the civilians lose their food.
* **Transition Trigger:** Skritch appears at the end of the warehouse row, on a small stone platform, watching the burning. Skritch is the Empire's overseer for the withdrawal. Skritch's presence is the named antagonist's entrance.

## 2. Universal Hooks

The four hooks are rewritten from the engine template to fit the Garegstown campaign. None are rolled. All four are active in this session as facts the party can discover.

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Warehouse Map).** The Empire quartermaster at Garegstown has a hand drawn map of the warehouse district, marked with the warehouses that contain the winter food stores. The map is worth 30 gp to the rebellion. Bounty equivalent: 30 gp for the map and the food stores saved.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Civilian Evacuation).** The Garegstown market is full of civilians who have not yet evacuated. The evacuation is led by a halfling named Ulna, who runs a safehouse on the road to Paradiso (the same Ulna from Bodrik's quarry map). Distress call: Ulna is alive, the civilians are alive, and the smoke is coming.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Empire's Hidden Stockpile).** The Empire command post has a hidden stockpile of weapons and armor in a locked basement. The stockpile is what the Empire is taking with it. The unsealing is the basement, and the stockpile is a Session 8 thread.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Withdrawal Route).** The Empire soldiers are withdrawing along the cliffside road, the same road the party will need to take to leave Garegstown. The ambush is the withdrawal route, and the party can either avoid the route or take it from the Empire.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

Ten concrete facts about Garegstown, the scorched earth withdrawal, Skritch, the Empire command post, and the civilians. Each is discoverable in play through the listed check or social action.

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Garegstown's warehouse district was built on a filled cove in the second age of the dwarven calendar. The warehouses are stone foundations with wooden upper floors. The stone foundations survive the fire. The wooden upper floors do not. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)** or asking any Garegstown dwarf.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The Empire command post's basement is below the water table. The basement floods when the tide is high. The flood is a Tier 1 environmental hazard. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** when entering the basement.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Skritch's Pack Lord's Snarl ability is interruptible per the wave inherited canon from module-2. The interrupt is a successful DC 13 Charisma (Performance) counter shout, a Silence spell, or a successful DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check to identify the cue. Sunlight Sensitivity is inherited from the kobold base. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** the first time Skritch opens his mouth to snarl, or by asking Pip.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** The Empire soldiers in the warehouse district are not volunteers. They are conscripts, and they are afraid of Skritch. If Skritch is neutralized, the conscripts will stop the burning and surrender. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Insight)** at the warehouse row, or by noticing the conscripts' lack of zeal.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** The Empire command post has a back door to a small boat cove. The cove is the Empire's extraction point. The back door is the named antagonist's escape route. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** at the command post, or by following Skritch.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The Empire is withdrawing by sea. A single Empire transport, the *Sable Wake* (per the wave inherited canon from Session 2), is anchored in the cove. The transport departs at noon. The party has until noon to stop the withdrawal. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** on the cliff, or by asking a civilian.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The Empire command post's basement lock is a dwarven puzzle lock, three rotating rings, the combination is the current dwarven calendar date. Skritch knows the date. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the lock, or by capturing Skritch.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Ulna the safehouse keeper has been running the road to Paradiso safehouse for two years. Ulna's husband, Thorek the wagoneer, was executed at Session 6 in Paradiso. Ulna does not know yet. Discovery: asking Ulna, or **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** in the market.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** The Empire command post has a loose flagstone under the desk. The flagstone covers a small iron box containing 35 gp, the warehouse map (Secret 1), and a sealed letter from the Empire garrison commander at Braaken ordering the withdrawal. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** in the command post, or by searching Skritch.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The Empire withdrawal is not the Empire leaving Draega. The withdrawal is a tactical repositioning to the mountain gate. The Empire will return to Garegstown in the spring, with a larger force. The withdrawal is a delay, not a victory. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the warehouse map, or by reading the garrison commander's letter.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

The four keyed areas are mapped to the session's real locations. The first is the warehouse district, the main fight scene. The second is the cliffside road, the environmental hazard. The third is the market, the civilian evacuation. The fourth is the Empire command post, the session's Boss Sanctum.

### Area 1: The Warehouse District (Entry Point)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. by 60 ft. stone and wood warehouse district at the base of the cliff, with ten warehouses arranged in two rows of five. Lighting: bright morning sun filtered through smoke. Smell: burning wood, lamp oil, the iron tang of the cliff. Sound: the crackle of fire, the shouts of Empire soldiers, the murmur of the market beyond the smoke.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The warehouses are stone foundations with wooden upper floors. The stone survives, the wood does not. (2) The cliff face is to the north, providing 3/4 cover from the warehouses.
* **Encounter (Empire Soldiers Burning the Warehouses):**
  * **4 humans in heavy armor** (AC 16 (chain mail and shield), HP 11 (2d8+2), Speed 30 ft.; Attack: longsword, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d8+2 slashing damage; or torch, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 fire damage; Multiattack: one attack with each weapon).
  * Minions: **4 kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight).
* **Investigative DCs:**
  * **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception):** the Empire soldiers are conscripts, not volunteers, and they are not setting the fires with enthusiasm.
  * **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation):** the warehouses have stone foundations that survive the fire. The wooden upper floors are what burn.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp from the warehouse district itself. The party can save at least one warehouse by putting out the fires with sand and water, but the wooden upper floor is lost. The stone foundation can be rebuilt.

### Area 2: The Cliffside Road (Hazard Chamber)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 30 ft. wide stone road cut into the cliff face, with a 50 ft. drop to the sea on the south side. Lighting: bright morning sun filtered through smoke. Smell: burning wood, the salt of the sea, the iron tang of the cliff. Sound: the crackle of fire, the shouts of Empire soldiers, the crash of waves below.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The road is 30 ft. wide, providing ample room for a group fight. (2) The cliff edge has a low stone wall, 3 ft. tall, providing 1/2 cover but no protection from a hard shove.
* **Trap / Environmental Hazard (The Burning Warehouses):**
  * **Trigger:** A creature on the road when a warehouse's wooden upper floor collapses and rolls onto the road.
  * **Detection:** **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** to hear the creak of the collapsing wood.
  * **Disarm:** not applicable. The hazard is the burning warehouses.
  * **Failure Effect:** **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** for any creature within 10 ft. of the collapse, taking **2d10 (11) fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success. The creature is then on the road with burning debris.
* **Monsters / Sentinels:** None in this area. The road is the Empire's withdrawal route, and the Empire soldiers are at the warehouse district and the command post. The hazard is the environment.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp from the road itself.

### Area 3: The Garegstown Market (Puzzle or Lore Hub)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. by 100 ft. cobblestone market square at the base of the cliff, with stalls, awnings, and a small fountain at the center. Lighting: bright morning sun filtered through smoke. Smell: bread, fish, lamp oil, the smoke of the burning warehouses. Sound: the murmur of civilians, the shouts of Ulna, the distant crackle of fire.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The fountain at the center provides 3/4 cover. (2) The market has a single exit to the cliffside road and a single exit to the caverns above.
* **Interactive Puzzle / Obstacle (The Civilian Evacuation):**
  * **The Mechanism:** The civilians need to evacuate. Ulna is leading the evacuation, but Ulna does not have enough time. The party has three workable paths. (a) The party helps Ulna lead the evacuation, requiring a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check to convince the civilians to move quickly. (b) The party creates a distraction, requiring a **DC 13 Charisma (Performance)** check to draw the civilians' attention while Ulna leads them out. (c) The party blocks the road to the warehouses, requiring a **DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** check to push a market stall into the road.
  * **The Solution:** All three paths work. The civilians evacuate to the caverns above. The market is empty by noon.
  * **Failure Consequence:** If the party does not help, the civilians do not evacuate in time. The smoke reaches the market and the civilians are trapped. The party can still evacuate them, but the session's emotional cost is higher.
* **Encounter (Social):** Ulna the safehouse keeper, a halfling woman in a stained apron, leading the evacuation. Ulna does not yet know that her husband Thorek was executed at Session 6. Ulna will learn this in a future session. Ulna is a recurring NPC.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp from the market. The party can purchase gear at standard prices or accept donations from grateful civilians.

### Area 4: The Empire Command Post (The Boss Sanctum)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 30 ft. by 25 ft. stone command post on a small stone platform at the end of the warehouse row, with a single door, a single window, and a basement hatch. Lighting: bright morning sun, with the smoke from the warehouses filtering through the window. Smell: lamp oil, paper, the iron tang of the cliff. Sound: the crackle of fire, the low conversation of the Empire detail, the distant crash of waves.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The command post has a back door to a small boat cove (Secret 5). (2) The basement hatch is below the water table and floods at high tide.
* **Boss Encounter:**
  * **Kobold Captain Skritch** (AC 13, HP 22, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: shortsword, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d6+3 piercing damage; Special Ability: Pack Lord's Snarl, recharge 5-6, target one creature within 30 ft., DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or Frightened until the end of Skritch's next turn; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight).
  * Minions: **four kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics; Sunlight Sensitivity).
* **Morale / Tactics:** Skritch is the named antagonist and the session's build to. Skritch flees at less than 20% HP (5 or fewer HP) and attempts to escape through the back door to the boat cove. The four kobold minions rout immediately if Skritch flees. Skritch surrenders if reduced to 5 or fewer HP and cornered. Skritch uses Pack Lord's Snarl on a cluster of three or more party members to scatter the formation. Skritch's Pack Lord's Snarl is denied for the round if the party disrupts the verbal command (Secret 3, per the wave inherited canon from module-2).
* **Boss Sanctum Substitution:** The session's "Boss Sanctum" is the Empire command post. The named antagonist is Skritch, recurring from Session 0.5 and Session 2. The session's tension is the warehouse burning, the civilian evacuation, and Skritch's command. The party can always end the session with Skritch captured or fled.

## 6. Encounter Math Breakdown

The session fits inside a 2,400 XP daily budget for four level 2 characters. The day has one required combat encounter, one environmental hazard, one social scene, and one Boss Sanctum fight.

* **Encounter 1 (Area 1, Warehouse District):** 4 humans in heavy armor (4 × 100 = 400 XP) + 4 kobolds (4 × 25 = 100 XP). Adjusted XP with encounter multiplier x2 for mixed foes = 500 × 2 = 1,000 XP (Hard). The conscripts surrender if Skritch is neutralized, dropping the encounter to 200 XP (Easy).
* **Encounter 2 (Area 2, Cliffside Road):** 0 creatures. Tier 1 trap (burning warehouse collapse), 2d10 fire at DC 12. Trap XP: 20 XP (Trivial).
* **Encounter 3 (Area 3, Garegstown Market):** 0 creatures. Social scene. No XP.
* **Encounter 4 (Area 4, Empire Command Post):** 1 Skritch (450 XP) + 4 kobolds (100 XP). Adjusted XP with encounter multiplier x2 for mixed foes = 550 × 2 = 1,100 XP (Hard). Skritch flees at 5 HP, dropping the effective encounter to roughly 350 XP (Medium).
* **Day Total (all four areas to combat, no skips):** 1,000 + 20 + 0 + 1,100 = 2,120 XP. Hard, inside the 2,400 XP budget. This is the worst case.
* **Day Total (realistic, conscripts surrender, Skritch flees):** 200 + 20 + 0 + 350 = 570 XP. Easy to Medium, inside budget.
* **Bend:** The session's day total is 570 to 2,120 XP, inside the 2,400 XP budget. The session is a warehouse burning with a named antagonist at the command post. The budget is honored in both the realistic and worst cases.
