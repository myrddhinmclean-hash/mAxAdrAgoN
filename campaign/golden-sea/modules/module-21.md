# The Outer Glacial Gates of Glazhenge

**Level:** 7
**Setting:** The cyclopean glacial ice walls, frozen moat trenches, and obsidian-reinforced iron battlements of outer Glazhenge, battered by sub-zero blizzard gales.
**Main Threat:** Blood-bound Frost Giant Thralls, heavy glacial ballista batteries, sub-zero hypothermia hazards, and crumbling ice ramparts.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 26, following a grueling six-day march through blizzard-swept highland passes, the allied liberation army arrives before the cyclopean fortress city of Glazhenge. Carved directly into a massive vertical glacier, Glazhenge serves as the final terrestrial bastion protecting the Vampire King's mountain domain. The party spearheads the assault against the outer defensive perimeter: crossing a frozen moat seeded with razor ice spikes, dodging automated glacial artillery, and confronting two massive Frost Giant Thralls guarding the iron portcullis. Utilizing siege explosives and the elemental power of **W1** and **W2**, the party shatters the outer ice gate, establishing an allied foothold within the lower city.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> A blinding wall of white ice crystals screams off the high glacier face, driving the temperature thirty degrees below freezing. Ahead, through the swirling gale, the Outer Gate of Glazhenge rises eighty feet into the storm: a massive arch of black iron and solid blue glacier ice, flanked by twin obsidian bastions that bristle with heavy ice-bolt ballistas. At the foot of the frozen drawbridge, two towering frost giants clad in spiked star-iron armor grip massive greataxes that crackle with dark necrotic frost, their eyes burning with empty crimson light as they step forward to crush the allied vanguard.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** 2 Blood-Bound Frost Giant Thralls defend the outer gate drawbridge, supported by 2 Glacial Ballista Batteries on the parapets.

* **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the giant thralls, disable the ballista winches (**DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** or **Thieves' Tools**), and breach the frozen iron portcullis within 4 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The iron portcullis shatters under a coordinated siege blast, collapsing the ice archway and granting entry into the lower outer wards.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Glazhenge was originally sculpted by ancient frost elves as a temple to the northern auroras before the Empire fortified its bastions. Discovery: **DC 12 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** Stepping onto the thin ice over the moat triggers a collapse, dropping victims 30 ft. into razor spikes dealing **4d10 piercing and cold damage** (DC 16 Dex save halves). Discovery: **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Frost Giant Thralls are vulnerable to radiant and fire damage; focusing radiant attacks from **W2** causes their necrotic shielding to violently backfire for **4d10 radiant damage**. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Severing the central blood-communion tether on the gatehouse roof causes the giant thralls to enter a berserk rage, attacking their imperial handlers. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A thermal meltwater flume on the western bastion allows silent infiltration into the lower gatehouse mechanism room. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** The northern blizzard will intensify into a lethal whiteout in two hours, halting all offensive operations if the gatehouse is not captured. Discovery: **DC 12 Wisdom (Survival)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The drawbridge counterweights are frozen in solid blue ice; applying concentrated fire or dwarven thermite melts the gears instantly. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The inner city of Glazhenge is protected by an impenetrable blood-ward that can only be shattered by the combined resonance of the Four Boons. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked iron chest inside the gatehouse commander's solar holds 220 gp, two potions of superior healing, and a scroll of cone of cold. Discovery: **DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Intercepted dispatches confirm that Verrick has retreated to the Inner HQ Gates, preparing the final conjunction ritual. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Gatehouse Assault

### Area 1: The Frozen Moat & Drawbridge (The Outer Breach)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. wide frozen moat trench filled with blue ice boulders and razor spikes, swept by blizzard winds.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 2 Blood-Bound Frost Giants (AC 15, HP 138 each, greataxe +9, 3d12+6 slashing plus 1d8 necrotic) + 2 Glacial Ballistas (**4d10 cold damage**, DC 15 Dex save).
* **Tactical Objective:** Cross the moat, neutralize the giants, and lower the frozen drawbridge.

### Area 2: The Outer Parapet & Ballista Towers (The High Ground)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 20 ft. wide stone wallwalk eighty feet above the moat, covered in slippery black ice.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 4 Imperial Winter Marksmen (AC 15, HP 45 each) + 2 Inquisitor Lieutenants (AC 16, HP 55 each).
* **Hazard:** Strong winds give disadvantage on ranged attacks beyond 30 feet unless using heavy siege weapons.

### Area 3: The Lower Barbican Courtyard (The Foothold)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. square stone courtyard filled with abandoned supply sleds and fortified barricades.
* **Securing the Lower Ward (Gated Event):** The allied vanguard enters the lower city, fortifying the outer gate to withstand the blizzard and staging the assault on the Inner HQ Gates.
