# Garegstown, Escape Through the Caverns

**Level:** 2
**Setting:** The Garegstown caverns, a smugglers' network above the cliffside docks, with the upper camp, the narrow traverse, the wagoneer's alcove, and the lower cavern exit
**Main Threat:** The caverns themselves, a Tier 1 environmental hazard, and a kobold pursuit detail sent from the docks
**Adventure Summary:** The party must traverse the caverns from the upper camp to the lower exit, where the wagoneer waits with W3 and the road to Paradiso. The traverse costs a dwarf scout's life and the party's grip on the schedule. If the party does not move, the second relief column arrives at the docks and the cavern exit is sealed.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The upper cavern smells of wet stone and old smoke, and the air is colder than the sea air by ten degrees and drier by half. A single tallow candle on a stone ledge throws long shadows across the cavern wall, and the wall is covered in old chalk marks, and the chalk marks are the wagoneer's route notes from the last six months. Borrik the scout is sitting with his back to the wall and his short sword across his knees, and Borrik is sharpening the sword with a stone, and the stone is the only sound in the cavern that is not water.

* **Active Obstacle:** The cavern traverse. The party must cross the narrow traverse, a 60 ft. long stretch of cavern with a collapsed floor, to reach the wagoneer's alcove. The traverse is a Tier 1 hazard. Borrik the scout will lead the way and hold the line if kobolds pursue.
* **Tactical Objective:** Cross the traverse, reach the wagoneer's alcove, secure W3, exit the lower cavern. The party has 3 in game days (about 6 real world hours) before the second relief column arrives. Failure trigger: the traverse collapse blocks the route and the party must find another way, which costs time.
* **Transition Trigger:** Borrik's short sword clatters to the stone floor. The traverse has begun.

## 2. Universal Hooks

The four hooks are rewritten from the engine template to fit the Garegstown campaign. None are rolled. All four are active in this session as facts the party can discover.

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Wagoneer's Map).** Thorek the wagoneer has a hand drawn map of the caverns, marking safe paths, flooded sections, and one side exit that leads to the Garegstown market. The map is worth 25 gp to the rebellion. Bounty equivalent: 25 gp for the map.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as Borrik's Last Letter).** Borrik the scout has a sealed letter to his sister in FairWhether, to be delivered if Borrik does not return. The letter is in Borrik's belt pouch. Distress call: Borrik's sister will never read it unless the party delivers it.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Collapsed Floor).** The narrow traverse was a smuggler's corridor before a winter flood collapsed the floor. The collapse revealed a small side chamber with three stone casks of Empire wine. The unsealing is the casks, and the wine is a Session 6 thread.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Kobold Pursuit).** A kobold pursuit detail of four kobolds has entered the caverns from the docks, following the party's trail. The detail is led by a kobold named Vren, the same Vren from Session 3 (if disbanded) or a different kobold (if Vren was killed). The ambush is the pursuit, and Borrik will hold the line.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

Ten concrete facts about the Garegstown caverns, the wagoneer, W3, the road to Paradiso, and the second relief column. Each is discoverable in play through the listed check or social action.

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Garegstown caverns were carved as a smuggler's network in the second age of the dwarven calendar, when Garegstown was a free port. The Empire sealed the surface entrances and used the docks as a customs post. The caverns fell out of use. The wagoneer reopened them. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)** or asking Thorek.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The narrow traverse has a collapsed floor in the middle. The collapse revealed three stone casks in a side chamber, and the casks are sitting on a thin ledge over a 30 ft. drop. The ledge will fail if more than 200 pounds is placed on it at once. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** when entering the traverse.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The kobold pursuit detail is led by Vren (or a replacement kobold). Vren is afraid of Skritch. If the party mentions Skritch by name, Vren breaks off the pursuit. Sunlight Sensitivity is inherited from the kobold base. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** the first time the pursuit detail is visible, or by asking Pip (if Pip is present, which the module does not assume).
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Borrik the scout is a stonecutter from FairWhether, recruited by Bodrik at Session 3. Borrik's sister, Kelda, is a furrier in FairWhether. Borrik's death will be a Session 6 thread if the party returns to FairWhether, or a Session 7 thread if the party does not. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Insight)** at the upper camp, or by asking Borrik directly.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** The wagoneer's alcove has a side exit to the Garegstown market, through a small drainage channel. The channel is the wagoneer's emergency exit if the lower cavern is sealed. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** at the alcove, or Thorek shows the party.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The second relief column arrives at the Garegstown docks at Day 12, 3 in game days from the start of the session. The column will seal the cavern entrances with rubble and the party will be trapped in the caverns if they do not exit by Day 12. Discovery: **DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the wagoneer's route notes, or by asking Thorek.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The narrow traverse has a rope bridge that the wagoneer installed. The rope bridge is 30 ft. long and is anchored to two stone pillars. One anchor is loose. The party can tighten the anchor with a successful **DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** check, or cross without tightening and risk the bridge slipping. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** on the bridge, or by testing the anchor.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Thorek the wagoneer has been running the Garegstown caverns for the rebellion for two years. Thorek's cover is a market vendor in Garegstown. Thorek's wife, Ulna, runs a safehouse on the road to Paradiso. Discovery: asking Thorek, or **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** in the alcove.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** The wagoneer's alcove has a loose flagstone under the wagon's rear wheel. The flagstone covers a small iron box containing 25 gp, the wagoneer's emergency fund, and a list of Empire garrison rotations at Garegstown. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** in the alcove, or Thorek shows the party.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The Empire garrison at Garegstown has received reinforcements. The reinforcements are EoE specialists, not Empire regulars. The EoE Handler is Verrick, who was at the docks at Session 4. Verrick has been seen at the Garegstown market, in the same leather coat. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Investigation)** at the alcove, or by asking the wagoneer.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

The four keyed areas are mapped to the session's real locations. The first is the upper camp. The second is the narrow traverse, the session's main environmental hazard. The third is the wagoneer's alcove, the social and lore hub. The fourth is the lower cavern exit, the kobold pursuit and Borrik's last stand.

### Area 1: The Upper Cavern Camp (Entry Point)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 40 ft. by 30 ft. stone cavern with a single tallow candle, a small cold fire pit, and Borrik's gear piled against the wall. Lighting: dim, with the candle casting long shadows. Smell: wet stone, old smoke, the cold iron smell of Borrik's short sword. Sound: the drip of water, the slow scratch of Borrik's stone on the blade, the distant rumble of the sea through the cliff face.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The cold fire pit is a Tier 1 hazard if relit in a tight space. (2) The cavern has a single entrance to the narrow traverse and a single entrance to the upper cliff face, both visible from the camp.
* **Encounter:** None in this area. Borrik the scout is the only other person in the camp.
* **Investigative DCs:**
  * **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception):** the chalk marks on the wall are the wagoneer's route notes.
  * **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation):** the cold fire pit was relit within the last day, by someone other than Borrik.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp from the camp itself. Borrik's gear is worth 0 gp, but Borrik's short sword is a masterwork weapon worth 15 gp and is recovered after Borrik's death.

### Area 2: The Narrow Traverse (Hazard Chamber)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. long, 10 ft. wide stone corridor with a collapsed floor in the middle, revealing a 30 ft. drop to a lower cavern. Lighting: dim, with light filtering through the collapsed floor from below. Smell: wet stone, the musty smell of the casks in the side chamber, the iron tang of the rope bridge. Sound: the drip of water, the creak of the rope bridge, the distant echo of the party's footsteps.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The collapsed floor has a side chamber with three stone casks, on a thin ledge over the 30 ft. drop. (2) The rope bridge is 30 ft. long, anchored to two stone pillars, one anchor loose.
* **Trap / Environmental Hazard (The Rope Bridge):**
  * **Trigger:** A creature crossing the bridge without tightening the loose anchor, or a critical hit on the bridge's anchor.
  * **Detection:** **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** to notice the loose anchor.
  * **Disarm:** **DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** to tighten the anchor within 1 round.
  * **Failure Effect:** **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** for any creature on the bridge, taking **2d10 (11) bludgeoning damage** on a failed save from the 30 ft. fall, or half as much on a success. The creature is then on the lower cavern floor and must climb back up or find another way.
* **Monsters / Sentinels:** None. The traverse is empty. The threat is the environment.
* **Area Loot:** Three stone casks of Empire wine, worth 10 gp each if sold in Garegstown market. The casks are heavy. Bringing them costs 1 round of carry time each.

### Area 3: The Wagoneer's Alcove (Puzzle or Lore Hub)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 25 ft. by 20 ft. stone alcove with a covered wagon, a small lantern, and Thorek the wagoneer. Lighting: dim, with the lantern casting long shadows. Smell: lamp oil, the leather of the wagon cover, the stone-dust smell of Thorek's coat. Sound: the creak of the wagon, the low voice of Thorek, the distant drip of water.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The wagon's rear wheel is on a loose flagstone (Secret 9). (2) The alcove has a side exit to the Garegstown market through a small drainage channel.
* **Interactive Puzzle / Obstacle (W3 Explanation):**
  * **The Mechanism:** Thorek explains W3's purpose. W3 is a small leather pouch containing a folded square of treated canvas. When draped over a vessel and a command word is spoken, the vessel and its crew become invisible to scrying and to visual observation from more than 30 ft. away. The explanation is a social encounter, not a mechanical puzzle. The party has three questions they can ask Thorek about W3.
  * **The Solution:** The party can ask (a) what W3 does, (b) who made W3, and (c) what the limits of W3 are. Thorek answers all three truthfully, because Thorek trusts the liaison's judgment and the liaison trusts the party. The explanation takes 10 minutes of in game time and 10 minutes of real world time.
  * **Failure Consequence:** If the party does not ask all three questions, the explanation is incomplete and the party's use of W3 in later sessions is at the GM's discretion. The session does not require the party to ask all three.
* **Encounter (Social):** Thorek the wagoneer, a dwarf in a patched coat with a wide-brimmed hat. Thorek will answer questions, guide the party to the lower cavern exit, and reveal that the Empire garrison has been reinforced.
* **Area Loot:** 25 gp from the loose flagstone (Secret 9), plus the wagoneer's cavern map (Secret 1, 25 gp value). W3 is observed but not given to the party in this session per the wave brief. Thorek holds W3.

### Area 4: The Lower Cavern Exit (The Boss Sanctum)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 40 ft. by 30 ft. stone cavern at the base of the cliff, with a single exit to the road above and a small stream running through it. Lighting: dim, with daylight filtering through the exit. Smell: wet stone, the fresh water of the stream, the iron tang of the kobold pursuit's weapons. Sound: the drip of water, the rush of the stream, the padding of kobold feet on stone.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The exit is a 10 ft. by 10 ft. opening in the cliff face, with a wrought iron gate that Thorek has the key to. (2) The stream is 2 ft. deep and runs from the cavern to a small pool outside the exit.
* **Boss Encounter (Kobold Pursuit Detail):**
  * **Vren** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight).
  * Minions: **three kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics; Sunlight Sensitivity).
* **Morale / Tactics:** Vren is the same Vren from Session 3 (if disbanded) or a replacement kobold (if Vren was killed). If Vren is alive, Vren fears Skritch and breaks off the pursuit if Skritch is mentioned by name. The kobolds pursue from the upper cavern and engage at the lower exit. Borrik the scout holds the line at the traverse and dies defending the party's retreat. The kobolds break off the pursuit if Vren is reduced to 0 HP or if Borrik's death is observed by Vren.
* **Boss Sanctum Substitution:** The session's "Boss Sanctum" is the lower cavern exit. The encounter is the kobold pursuit, not a named antagonist. The session's named loss is Borrik the scout. The party's tension is the traverse, the wagoneer's explanation, and Borrik's last stand. The party can always end the session at the exit, on the road to Paradiso.

  * *Combat Behavior:* Verrick does not fight. Verrick observes. Verrick is at the Garegstown market, not in the caverns. Has AC 12, HP 11.

* **Day Total (Vren breaks off at the pursuit):** 20 XP. Trivial, well inside budget.
* **Bend:** The session's day total is 20 to 220 XP, inside the 2,400 XP budget. The session is a cavern traverse with a named loss, not a four encounter dungeon. The budget is honored by a factor of 11 to 120.
