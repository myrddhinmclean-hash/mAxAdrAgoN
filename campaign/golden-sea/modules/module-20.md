# The Foundry Assault and the Tithe Crucible of Bernax

**Level:** 6
**Setting:** The roaring blast furnaces, molten slag rivers, and iron catwalks of the Grand Central Foundry of Bernax.
**Main Threat:** Massive clockwork forge constructs, superheated molten metal hazards, fanatical EoE alchemists, and crushing piston presses.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 20, the party emerges from the ore cart tunnels into the heart of the Grand Foundry of Bernax. Here, imperial smiths and EoE alchemists smelt blood-infused star-iron to forge the dark armaments for the Vampire King's legions. In the central smelting hall, the party discovers the **Tithe Crucible**, a monstrous occult furnace that converts life-debt souls recorded by the **Tithe Ledger** into blackened war plate. Battling through automated mechanical forge constructs and Verrick's elite alchemical templars, the party shatters the crucible's coolant valves, causing a catastrophic thermal overload that shatters the occult machinery. Liberating the city's metalworkers, the party opens the northern gates, clearing the military highway to Glazhenge.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Heat hits like an iron hammer as the ore cart smashes through the timber buffer of the smelting floor, derailing onto a platform of red-hot iron plates. The Grand Foundry of Bernax is a cathedral of fire: five roaring blast furnaces thirty feet tall pour glowing orange slag into stone flumes below, while massive overhead piston hammers shake the foundation every six seconds. In the center of the hall, fifty paces ahead, suspended above a pit of boiling liquid metal, the Tithe Crucible glows with pulsating crimson runes, guarded by a towering iron forge golem whose molten furnace chest glows white with lethal heat.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** 1 Iron Forge Golem and 2 Alchemical Templars defend the catwalk leading to the Tithe Crucible's primary emergency release valve.

* **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the forge golem, sever the three occult blood-feed siphons (**DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** or **Strength (Athletics)**), and trigger the thermal overload within 4 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The third blood-feed pipe ruptures in a hiss of pressurized green steam, causing the runes on the crucible to crack and discharge blinding arcs of radiant energy.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The grand foundries were originally built over dormant volcanic fissures by dwarven smiths before imperial alchemists corrupted the vents. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** Stepping onto the molten slag flumes deals **4d10 fire damage** per round (DC 15 Dex save halves). Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The Iron Forge Golem is vulnerable to extreme thermal shock; dousing its molten chest core in cryogenic coolant or **W1** water deals **6d10 cold damage** and stuns it for one round. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Dropping the main foundry steam valve releases thousands of gallons of boiling water into the garrison barracks, forcing two hundred imperial reinforcements to surrender. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** An exhaust chimney behind Furnace Three leads directly out onto the northern military highway toward Glazhenge. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** In twenty minutes, the crucible will complete the forging of fifty cursed blood-iron armors, unleashing an elite strike battalion against the liberated docks. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The crucible overload mechanism requires simultaneous clockwise rotation of the twin coolant release wheels on opposite sides of the gantry. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (Scheduled Event):** Although the local crucible is shattered, the primary **Tithe Ledger** remains active and intact elsewhere, bound to the Vampire King's grand design. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked fireproof safe inside the foundry master's office holds 180 gp, a masterwork dwarven war hammer, and a potion of invulnerability. Discovery: **DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Intercepted dispatches confirm that the entire imperial remnant and the Vampire King's elite legions have fortified the outer gates of Glazhenge. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Crucible Battle

### Area 1: The Smelting Floor & Piston Catwalks (The Approach)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 150 ft. by 80 ft. multi-tiered industrial chamber crisscrossed by iron catwalks and molten slag channels.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 2 Alchemical Templars (AC 16, HP 65 each, flame strike spells) + 4 Foundry Guards (AC 16, HP 38 each) + piston drop hazard.
* **Tactical Objective:** Secure the high gantry and clear the approach to the central crucible platform.

### Area 2: The Tithe Crucible Platform (The Boss Arena)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 50 ft. circular iron platform suspended forty feet above a boiling slag basin.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):**
  * **1 Iron Forge Golem** (CR 7, AC 17, HP 115, slam +8 to hit, 2d10+5 bludgeoning plus 2d6 fire, fire absorption, steam breath).
  * **The Overload Objective:** Survive the golem's attacks while rotating the dual coolant wheels to detonate the crucible core.

### Area 3: The North Foundry Gate & Highway Depot (The Liberation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Massive iron double gates leading directly out of the mountain gorge onto the snowbound northern plateau.
* **Securing the Path (Gated Event):** The liberated metalworkers seize the city armories, arming three hundred insurgent dwarven warriors to secure Bernax and open the road to Glazhenge.
