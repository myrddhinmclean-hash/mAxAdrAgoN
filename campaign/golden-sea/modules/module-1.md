# The FairWhether Revolt and the Three Watches

**Level:** 1
**Setting:** The stone streets, bustling central market, damp waterfront storehouses, and dockside alleys of the occupied port city of FairWhether.
**Main Threat:** Empire occupation patrols, garrison informers, a trained sniper, and a predatory shrike hound.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 1, during an oppressive Empire Day celebration, the party intervenes when garrison guards harass the lamp merchant Durgan Emberwick and seize his daughter. The resulting scuffle ignites an oil fire, drives off the guards, and draws the dwarven fire brigade, enlisting the fugitive party into the local rebellion. On Day 2, the party completes three critical security watches across FairWhether: catching a spy at the rebel storehouse, guarding Cpt. T's fishing craft, and defending a mysterious incognito quartermaster from a shrike hound and sniper. The session closes at dusk with the party reporting new Empire naval arrivals and planning the dawn dock assault.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The market square of FairWhether smells of roast mutton, wet sheep fleece, and bitter coal smoke from the garrison braziers. It is Empire Day, and red banners bearing the imperial sun hang limply from the wooden eaves of every merchant stall. Beneath the stone awning of an oil shop, four kobold conscripts in scaled leather have cornered the dwarf merchant Durgan Emberwick, knocking over brass lanterns while a human sergeant drags the merchant's young daughter toward the garrison cart. The crowd stands frozen under the spears of the watch, watching the stones slick with spilled whale oil, waiting for someone to move.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** An Empire sergeant (human guard) and four kobold conscripts are dragging Durgan Emberwick's daughter toward a transport cart while kicking over oil drums. Spilled lamp oil is spreading across a 20 ft. radius around the stall, creating an ignition hazard.

* **Tactical Objective:** Subdue or drive off the sergeant and conscripts, secure Durgan's daughter, and keep the spreading oil from igniting the wooden tenement blocks.

* **Transition Trigger:** An overturned brazier sparks against the oil pool, sending a wall of yellow flame roaring toward the canvas awnings. The sound of copper fire horns echoes as the dwarven volunteer brigade charges into the square.

## 2. Universal Hooks

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Merchant's Gratitude).** Durgan Emberwick offers 25 gp in trade goods and safe shelter in his cellar to anyone who shields his family from the garrison.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Storehouse Runner).** A breathless dwarf runner arrives at the cellar safehouse, reporting that an Empire spy has located the rebellion's hidden dry provisions storehouse.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Waterfront Vessels).** Cpt. T requires armed guards along the lower slips where three fishing boats are being fitted for covert night passage.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The High Chimney Sentry).** A covert sniper team accompanied by an exotic tracking beast establishes an overwatch perch above the harbor transit exchange.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** FairWhether was founded as a seasonal dwarven fishing settlement before the Empire paved the upper plazas and imposed blackout curfews. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The storehouse roof joists are heavily charred from an old blaze; applying heavy concussive force to the upper beam will drop debris onto the floor below. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The storehouse spy carries a forged dock master's pass; confronting him with his real name (Garruk's courier) shatters his composure, giving advantage on social checks. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Pip, the young kobold conscript wearing a crooked red armband, deliberately misses with his sling when ordered to fire upon civilians. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A drainage culvert beneath Durgan's lamp cellar connects directly to the lower storehouse alley, avoiding street checkpoints. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** or asking Durgan.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** Three heavy Empire warships have just dropped anchor in the outer roadstead, preparing to land marine reinforcements at tomorrow's dawn. Discovery: **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** or observing the harbor with Cpt. T.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The rebel signal lanterns use blue glass shutters to designate safe alleys; displaying green indicates an active patrol. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** in the storehouse.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The incognito quartermaster under escort is a foreign logistics specialist whose travel papers are personally countersigned by high rebel leadership. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** while observing his mannerisms.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A loose masonry block beneath Cpt. T's center mooring ring conceals 30 gp and two vials of antitoxin. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The Empire garrison commander has ordered all dock warehouses sealed at midnight in preparation for martial inspections. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** from tavern chatter.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Three Watches

### Area 1: Central Market & Durgan's Shop (Day 1 Scene)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. by 60 ft. cobblestone plaza crowded with wooden produce stalls, canvas canopies, and scattered brass oil lanterns.
* **Encounter (Market Scuffle):**
  * **1 Empire Sergeant** (AC 16 (chain mail), HP 11, longsword +4 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing).
  * **4 Kobold Conscripts** (including Pip) (AC 12, HP 5 each, dagger +4 to hit, 1d4+2 piercing, sling +4 to hit).
* **Environmental Hazard (Spilled Lamp Oil):** Entering the oil slick requires a **DC 10 Dexterity saving throw** or fall prone. If ignited, the pool deals **1d10 fire damage** to creatures starting their turn in the area.

### Area 2: The Grain Storehouse (Day 2, Watch 1)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 50 ft. by 30 ft. timber warehouse stacked with flour sacks, grain bins, and hanging cargo tackle.
* **Tactical Encounter:** The party must corner and capture an Empire infiltrator searching through supply ledgers.
* **Encounter:** **1 Empire Spy** (AC 13, HP 16, shortsword +4 to hit, hand crossbow +4 to hit) plus **2 Kobold Lookouts** (AC 12, HP 5 each).

### Area 3: Cpt. T's Moorings (Day 2, Watch 2)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Low wooden jetty with three weathered fishing vessels tied to iron bollards, shrouded in tidal fog.
* **Tactical Encounter:** Preventing an Empire patrol boat from boarding and searching Cpt. T's lead vessel.
* **Encounter:** **3 Empire Marines** (AC 14 (scale mail), HP 9 each) attempting to cut the mooring lines.

### Area 4: The Chimney Intersection & Safehouse (Day 2, Watch 3 — The Climax)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Narrow stone intersection overlooked by three story slate roofs and brick industrial chimneys.
* **The VIP:** The incognito quartermaster (Glorinath d'Braaken, presented simply as a quiet quartermaster clad in a traveler's hood reviewing tidal rosters) is being escorted across the intersection between safehouses.
* **The Tactical Hazard (The Shrike Hound):**
  * An Empire bred **shrike hound** (AC 13 (natural armor), HP 19, bite +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing, Mimicry).
  * *Vocal Mimicry:* The shrike hound lurks in the alley shadows, mimicking the shrill cry of a lost child calling for help in an attempt to lure the VIP away from the escort and into the open street.
  * *Tactical Objective:* Detect the false call (**DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** or **Perception**) and slay the beast before it drags the quartermaster into the sniper's line of sight.
* **The Sniper Overwatch:**
  * **1 Empire Sniper** (AC 14 (studded leather), HP 13, heavy crossbow +5 to hit, 1d10+3 piercing) perched 40 ft. up on the slate chimney roof.
  * If the shrike hound is eliminated or the VIP kept in full cover, the sniper is denied clean line of sight and must retreat after firing two wild warning bolts.
* **The Collar Plant:** Around the shrike hound's neck rests a heavy, dull grey iron collar stamped with a sharp geometric eye sigil. No NPC in FairWhether recognizes the mark; Durgan inspects it, shrugs, and notes it is unusual imperial forge work.

## 5. NPC Roster

* **Durgan Emberwick** (dwarf, he/him, rebel quartermaster)
  * *Drive:* Organize logistics for the dock assault, protect the safehouse, keep the party supplied. Has AC 10, HP 9.
  * *Appearance and Voice:* Broad shoulders, a leather apron stained with soot, and an auburn beard plaited with brass wire. Voice booming and warm.
* **Glorinath d'Braaken (Incognito VIP)** (elf, he/him, prince in hiding)
  * *Drive:* Review tide charts, remain unrecognized, ensure the rebellion secures the boats for the crossing. Has AC 15, HP 30.
  * *Appearance and Manner:* Clad in a faded wool travel cloak, hood drawn forward. Speaks sparingly in a soft, cultivated tone; continually references harbor charts with tidal tables.
* **Pip** (kobold, he/him, reluctant conscript)
  * *Drive:* Avoid punishment from the Empire sergeant while secretly looking for an opportunity to desert. Has AC 12, HP 5.
