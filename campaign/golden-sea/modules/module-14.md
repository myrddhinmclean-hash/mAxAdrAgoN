# The Siege of Braaken Citadel and the Liberation

**Level:** 4
**Setting:** The grand marble colonnades, vaulted throne room, and fortified inner redoubts of Braaken Citadel.
**Main Threat:** The EoE Handler, elite imperial templars, and the desperate inner guard of the occupation headquarters.
**Adventure Summary:** On the morning of Day 37, the allied forces storm the shattered gates of Braaken Citadel. The party breaches the lower keep, fighting through fanatical inquisitors to liberate Prince Glori from the dungeon cells and recover **W1** from the imperial reliquary. Reunited with his ancestral relic, Prince Glori leads the party into the grand throne room for the final confrontation against the EoE Handler. In a fierce, lightning-charged duel across the marble dais, Glori strikes down the Handler, avenging Mother Ashfall and severing EoE's grip on the regional command. While the local Handler Verrick slips away during the confusion, the remaining conventional garrison surrenders. Dwarven fire horns sound across the harbor as Braaken is declared liberated.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Dawn breaks cold and grey over the burning towers of Braaken, shining through the shattered stained-glass dome of the Citadel vestibule. Inside the great rotunda, thirty paces ahead, four imperial templars in blackened steel hold the grand marble staircase with leveled halberds, while the muffled sound of chanting echoes from the iron doors of the high throne room above. Behind the party, dwarven sappers with sledgehammers smash open the dungeon grating below the stairs, and Prince Glori emerges into the light, battered and in chains, his eyes blazing with righteous fury as he points toward the private reliquary chest beside the stair head.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** Four Imperial Templars (veteran guards) defend the grand staircase, guarding the locked bronze reliquary containing **W1 (the Tide-Walker Disc)**.

* **Tactical Objective:** Break the templar line, smash the reliquary lock (**DC 13 Thieves' Tools** or **Strength** check), and return W1 to Prince Glori within 3 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** Glori grasps the emerald surface of W1, shattering his iron manacles in a surge of elemental power as the throne room doors blast inward.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Citadel throne was carved from a single block of star-iron pulled from the northern mountain peaks by the founding clans. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The grand glass dome above the throne room is fractured; a concussive shock drops **3d8 piercing glass shards** across the central dais. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The Handler draws occult power from the black iron scepter; disarming or sundering the focus dispels their psychic shielding. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** or **Insight**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Presenting Prince Glori with W1 in hand causes all remaining garrison soldiers in the hall to lay down their spears. Discovery: **DC 10 Charisma (Intimidation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A servant's staircase behind the royal tapestry leads directly to the western postern gate through which Verrick escapes. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The Handler is three rounds away from detonating the subterranean wildfire caches to incinerate the upper city. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** from the chanting cadence.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The wildfire fuses can be severed in the cellar vestibule by turning the master flood valve, dousing the lines in river water. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** W1 hums with restorative vitality, granting Prince Glori resistance to necrotic and psychic damage during the duel. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A concealed compartment beneath the royal seat holds 120 gp in ancient royal mint and a signet ring of state. Discovery: **DC 13 Investigation**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** Dispatches on the Handler's desk reveal that retreating imperial forces are consolidating under the enigmatic command of the Vampire King in the north. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Throne Room Duel

### Area 1: The Grand Rotunda & Reliquary (The Breach)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. circular marble hall with twin curving staircases rising to a 30 ft. high gallery.
* **Combat Encounter:** 4 Imperial Templars (AC 18, HP 32 each, halberd +5 to hit, 1d10+3 slashing) + 1 Inquisitor Lieutenant (AC 15, HP 38).
* **The Reliquary Recovery:** Smashing the bronze strongbox on the landing restores **W1 (Tide-Walker Disc)** to Prince Glori, who immediately joins the vanguard with full combat statistics.

### Area 2: The Grand Throne Room (The Climactic Duel)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. by 50 ft. vaulted audience chamber flanked by star-iron pillars, terminating at a raised five-step marble dais.
* **The Final Confrontation:**
  * **Combatants:** **The EoE Handler** (CR 5 Occult Master, AC 16, HP 75, psychic blast +7 to hit, 3d8+4 psychic, legendary actions) + 2 Shadow Guard (AC 15, HP 30 each).
  * **The Duel's Shape (Assumption Log):** The party engages the Shadow Guard and disrupts the wildfire ritual, while Prince Glori charges the dais. Channeling the oceanic fury of W1, Glori parries the Handler's iron scepter, driving the occult commander back onto the marble steps. With a final sweep of his blade charged with tidal light, Prince Glori strikes down the Handler, ending the occupation's reign of terror.

### Area 3: Western Postern Gate & Lower Courtyard (The Retreat)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Fortified iron exit gate opening onto the inland military highway.
* **The Threads of Sequel:**
  * **Verrick's Escape:** As the Handler falls, local Handler Verrick slips through the crowd of surrendering guards, mounting a swift courser and fleeing north toward the frontier (destination unknown).
  * **The Evil Second Party's Departure (Ruling 39):** Watching from the high gallery with cold contempt, the evil mirror party witnesses the fall of the Handler. When the party turns to confront them, they melt away into the dispersing crowds, their grudge over W2 burning and unresolved, never aligning with the liberators.
