# The Courier Flight and ITEM X

**Level:** 2
**Setting:** The Golden Sea, the courier flight from FairWhether toward Braaken, on a fishing boat with 3 kobold guards and the rebellion cargo. The voyage takes the party across open sea, with a surveillance encounter, a social encounter on the boat, and a navigation puzzle.
**Main Threat:** The Empire's surveillance of the courier flight, in the form of the 3 kobold guards, the unknown pursuit, and the sea itself. The secondary threat is the navigation puzzle, which is the session's mechanical difficulty.
**Adventure Summary:** The courier flight departs on the same afternoon following the dock assault of module-2. The party transports ITEM X and the rebellion cargo to Braaken with the Multipass entrusted to them, navigating plans for the sea crossing while surveilled by Empire agents.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The Golden Sea is the color of old iron and the wind has been pushing the same low clouds for a day, and the clouds have not moved, but the boat has. A fishing boat is not made for this kind of sea, and the hull creaks at every wave, and the rigging sings a low note that the crew has stopped hearing. The only new sound is the kobold on the rail, and the kobold has been on the rail since dawn, and the kobold is watching the horizon.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** The Empire has 3 kobold guards on the boat, and the party does not know if the guards are conscripts or volunteers. The party has 3 rounds of in game time (about 6 real world minutes) to decide: talk to the guards, hide from the guards, or prepare for the sea. Failure trigger: the party fails to establish contact with the guards, and the guards become suspicious.

* **Tactical Objective:** Establish contact with the kobold guards, or hide from them, or both. The party has 3 rounds to act. Failure trigger: the guards become suspicious, and the voyage becomes a surveillance encounter.

* **Transition Trigger:** The first kobold guard speaks to the party. The kobold is the same conscript from Session 0.5 and Session 2, with the red armband pinned on wrong. The kobold is Pip, the recurring Act I NPC. Pip's presence is the wave inherited canon. The session opens with Pip on the rail.

## 2. Universal Hooks

The four hooks are rewritten from the engine template to fit the Golden Sea campaign. None are rolled. All four are active in this session as facts the party can discover.

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Rebellion Cargo).** The rebellion cargo is medical supplies, 300 gp value to the rebellion. The cargo is sealed in a strongbox on the boat's deck. Bounty equivalent: 300 gp for the cargo, if delivered to Braaken.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Unknown Pursuit).** The Empire has sent an unknown pursuit vessel to intercept the courier flight. The pursuit is a warship, or a fast boat, or a magic item, or a traitor. The distress call is the unknown pursuit, and the party does not know what it is. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the boat's charts, or by asking Pip.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Boat's Hold).** The boat's hold contains the rebellion cargo, the locked strongbox with ITEM X, and the Multipass. The hold is the unsealed ruin, and the party can access the hold at any time. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** on the hold's lock, or by asking the kobold guards.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Sea Fog).** The sea fog is a Tier 1 environmental hazard. The fog is thick, cold, and disorienting. The fog is the direct ambush, and the party must navigate through it to reach Braaken. Discovery: **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** when the fog sets in, or by asking the kobold guards.

They are afraid of the Empire's punishment, but they are also afraid of the rebellion's retaliation. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** the first time the kobold guards are visible, or by asking Pip.

Pip is the most visible of the three guards, and Pip is the one the party can win over. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Insight)** at the rail, or by asking Pip directly.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** The boat's hold has a hidden compartment under the rebellion cargo. The compartment contains a sealed letter from the rebellion's liaison to the party, with instructions for the sea crossing. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** under the cargo, or by asking the liaison.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The Empire's unknown pursuit is closing on the courier flight. The pursuit is a warship, or a fast boat, or a magic item, or a traitor. The party has 3 in-game days to break the surveillance or lose the cargo. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** on the horizon, or by asking Pip.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The boat's navigation puzzle is a star chart, with the stars of the Golden Sea marked on a brass plate. The chart has a missing star, and the missing star is the key to the navigation. The puzzle is a **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check, with the solution being the name of the missing star. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the brass plate, or by asking the kobold guards.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The kobold guards are conscripts, and they are afraid of the Empire's punishment. They are also afraid of the rebellion's retaliation. They are the same conscripts from Session 0.5 and Session 2, with the red armband pinned on wrong. Pip is the most visible of the three guards. ## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

The four keyed areas are mapped to the session's real locations. The first is the boat's deck, the entry point. The second is the boat's hold, the puzzle hub. The third is the sea, the environmental hazard. The fourth is the open water destination point.

### Area 1: The Boat's Deck (Entry Point)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 30 ft. by 15 ft. fishing boat with a single mast, a small cabin aft, and a main deck crowded with rebellion cargo, fishing nets, and 3 kobold guards. Lighting: bright overcast sky, with the deck in flat light. Smell: salt, fish, tar, the wet rope of the rigging. Sound: the slap of water, the creak of the mast, the low conversation of the crew, the distant drumbeat of the unknown pursuit.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The mast block is rotten (Secret 2) and is a structural hazard if the boat takes a hard turn. (2) The kobold guards are on the rail, watching the horizon, and the party can talk to them, hide from them, or prepare for the sea.
* **Encounter:** None in this area. The boat's deck is the party's position, the kobold guards are the party's contact or threat, and the unknown pursuit is the party's distant threat.
* **Investigative DCs:**
  * **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception):** the mast block is cracked at the keel.
  * **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation):** the kobold guards are conscripts, not volunteers, and they are afraid of the Empire's punishment.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp from the boat's deck. The rebellion cargo is medical supplies, 300 gp value to the rebellion, sealed. The locked strongbox contains ITEM X, not opened, not defined. The Multipass is in the party's hand.

### Area 2: The Boat's Hold (Puzzle Hub)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 15 ft. by 10 ft. hold with a single door, a single lantern, and the rebellion cargo stacked on the floor. Lighting: dim, with the lantern casting long shadows. Smell: salt, the leather of the cargo straps, the iron tang of the hidden compartment. Sound: the drip of water, the creak of the cargo straps, the distant drumbeat of the unknown pursuit.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The hidden compartment under the cargo is the secret passage (Secret 5), with a sealed letter from the liaison. (2) The false bottom under the cargo is the hidden vault (Secret 9), with 50 gp and a sealed letter.
* **Interactive Puzzle / Obstacle (The Star Chart Puzzle):** 
  * **The Mechanism:** The star chart is a brass plate with the stars of the Golden Sea marked, with a missing star. The missing star is the key to the navigation. The puzzle is a **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check, with the solution being the name of the missing star. The puzzle has three workable paths: (a) the party can solve the puzzle with a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check, (b) the party can ask the kobold guards for the missing star, with a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, (c) the party can ignore the puzzle and navigate by the sea fog, with a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check each round.
  * **The Solution:** All three paths work. The party can navigate by solving the puzzle, by asking the guards, or by navigating through the fog.
  * **Failure Consequence:** If the party fails the puzzle, asks the guards and fails the persuasion check, or fails the fog navigation, the party loses 1 in-game day of voyage time, and the unknown pursuit closes to 1 in-game day behind the courier flight.
* **Encounter (Social):** The kobold guards are the party's contact or threat, depending on the party's approach. Pip is the most visible of the three guards, and Pip is the one the party can win over. Pip has the red armband pinned on wrong, and Pip is the same conscript from Session 0.5 and Session 2. Pip's presence is the wave inherited canon.
* **Area Loot:** 50 gp from the false bottom (Secret 9), plus the sealed letter (Secret 9, worth 0 gp), plus the sealed letter from the liaison (Secret 5, worth 0 gp).

### Area 3: The Sea (Hazard Chamber)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** The Golden Sea is a 200 ft. wide stretch of water between the courier flight and the unknown pursuit. Lighting: bright overcast sky, with the sea in flat light. Smell: salt, the iron tang of the silt. Sound: the slap of water, the wind, the distant drumbeat of the unknown pursuit.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The water is cold (60 degrees Fahrenheit) and swimming is possible but exhausting. (2) The sea fog is a Tier 1 environmental hazard, thick, cold, and disorienting.
* **Trap / Environmental Hazard (The Sea Fog):**
  * **Trigger:** The sea fog sets in, thick, cold, and disorienting.
  * **Detection:** **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** to notice the fog setting in.
  * **Disarm:** Not applicable. The hazard is the fog.
  * **Failure Effect:** **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** at the end of each round in the fog, taking **2d10 (11) cold damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails three Constitution saving throws in the fog begins to drown and must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw** each round or fall to 0 HP.
* **Monsters / Sentinels:** None. The sea is empty of monsters on the surface. The unknown pursuit is below, or behind, or waiting.
* **Area Loot:** 0 gp. The sea has no loot.

### Area 4: Open Sea Route (The Climax / Destination Point)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** Open sea covered in rolling fog banks, with the fishing boat riding the swell. Lighting: flat grey light. Smell: cold salt mist. Sound: water breaking against the hull.
* **Tactical Layout:** The boat navigates through the fog toward the destination corridor while managing the pursuit pressure.
* **Encounter Math:** Social resolution with the conscripts or environmental navigation through the fog.
