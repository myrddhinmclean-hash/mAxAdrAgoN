# The Frozen Docks of Bernax

**Level:** 6
**Setting:** The black iron wharves, icebound canal basins, and steam-belching smelting works of the heavy industrial port city of Bernax.
**Main Threat:** Automated steam-powered harpoon turrets, heavily armored ironclad marine shock troops, and crushing harbor ice flows.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 18, having crossed the frozen mountain passes, the party launches an assault on the industrial harbor of Bernax. As the Empire's primary northern foundry city, Bernax is heavily industrialized, producing weapons and iron war-hulks for the Vampire King's armies. Advancing across the frozen canal ice under the concealing mist of **W3**, the party storms the main iron quay, disables automated steam harpoon batteries, and ambushes an armored EoE convoy transporting blood-tithe manifests connected to the **Tithe Ledger**. Securing the docks, the party cuts off maritime supply lines to the upper foundries, preparing for the assault on the central city.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Thick yellow sulfur smoke billows from towering iron smelting stacks along the water's edge, casting an oily sheen across the jagged sheets of harbor ice. The port of Bernax is a city of black iron and roaring furnaces, its docks bristling with three-story steam harpoon turrets that hiss with pressurized vapor. Out on the frozen canal, eighty paces ahead, four massive draft mammoths clad in riveted iron plates drag a convoy of reinforced steel wagons bearing the geometric eye sigil, escorted by a dozen ironclad halberdiers marching double time toward the upper foundry gates.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** An armored convoy escorted by 1 Ironclad Tribune and 4 Heavy Halberdiers is crossing the frozen harbor canal toward the foundry lift.

* **Tactical Objective:** Ambush the convoy across the harbor ice, secure the transport wagon containing the tithe manifests, and disable the harbor steam harpoon turret within 3 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The lead mammoth's harness is severed, halting the convoy in the center of the ice sheet as the automated alarm siren screams across the harbor.

## 2. Universal Hooks

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Metalworker's Accord).** The Free Foundry Guild offers 180 gp in star-iron ingots for the destruction of the central harbor smelter cranes.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Trapped Ice Fishermen).** Twenty local dock laborers are trapped beneath a collapsed ice shelf under fire from automated turrets.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Submerged Slag Conduit).** A thermal drainage flume beneath Pier Four provides a warm, dry infiltration path beneath the harbor battlements.
4. **The Direct Ambush (Gated Event).** Two ironclad steam sentinels emerge from the shipyard repair berths to ram the party's flank.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Bernax was constructed over rich deposits of magnetic iron ore, which powers the city's unique steam-driven defensive grid. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The harbor ice sheet will fracture if subjected to heavy fire damage, plunging creatures into sub-zero brine dealing **3d10 cold damage** (DC 15 Con save). Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The Ironclad Tribune's powered armor relies on exposed hydraulic steam tubes on his back; severing the lines paralyzes his movement. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Releasing the pressure valves on the dock boilers blinds the entire defensive line in thick steam, granting heavy obscurement. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** An ore cart rail line behind the coal sheds leads directly into the lower foundries of the upper city. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** In four hours, the heavy ironclad warship *Titan's Wake* will finish coaling and deploy its broadside batteries against the lower wharves. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** Overriding the steam turret control console requires a three-key bypass sequence matching the pressure gauges. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (Scheduled Event):** The **Tithe Ledger** continues to autonomously record every casualty and transaction across the northern theater, binding souls to the Vampire King's tribute quotas. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked steel coffer in the harbormaster's watchtower holds 160 gp, two potions of fire resistance, and the foundry blueprints. Discovery: **DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Captured convoy manifests reveal that Verrick is currently inside the central Grand Foundry, supervising the construction of an occult ritual furnace. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Dock Assault

### Area 1: The Icebound Canal Basin (The Convoy Ambush)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 200 ft. wide frozen canal basin flanked by towering iron slag walls and wooden mooring piers.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 1 Ironclad Tribune (AC 18, HP 75, heavy maul +7, 2d6+4 bludgeoning) + 4 Heavy Halberdiers (AC 16, HP 38 each) + 2 Draft Mammoths (AC 13, HP 126 each).
* **Tactical Objective:** Capture the tithe wagon intact before it reaches the reinforced foundry lift.

### Area 2: Pier Four & The Steam Harpoon Battery (The Defensive Grid)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. square elevated stone platform mounting a twin-barrel automated steam harpoon turret.
* **Combat & Hazard Encounter (Gated Event):** 2 Steam Sentinels (AC 17, HP 55 each, steam jet +6 to hit, 3d6 fire) + automated harpoon battery (**4d10 piercing damage**, DC 15 Dex save).

### Area 3: The Coal Yard & Ore Cart Tram (The Infiltration Corridor)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Expansive timber storage yard stacked thirty feet high with black anthracite coal and iron ore hoppers.
* **Securing the Ascent (Gated Event):** Rousing the enslaved foundry workers, clearing the ore tram line, and boarding the heavy iron ore carts to assault the upper city.
