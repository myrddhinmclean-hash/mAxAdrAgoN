# The Beach Landing at Imnia North Port

**Level:** 5
**Setting:** The wind-scoured shingle beaches, black iron tidal booms, and steep granite cliffs of Imnia North Port, flanked by fortified timber stockades and coastal gun emplacements.
**Main Threat:** Shore artillery batteries, barbed iron beach obstacles, elite coastal defense cohorts, and sudden tidal surges.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 4, following four days of coastal transit from Braaken, Cpt. T's refitted vessel arrives off the fortified shores of Imnia North Port. In response to the fall of Braaken, the imperial northern command has placed the coast under emergency martial lockdown, converting the fishing harbor into a barbed redoubt. Utilizing **W1** and **W3**, the party leads a daring amphibious landing across the wave-swept tidal flats. The party breaches the iron harbor boom, storms the cliffside cannon battery, and routs the garrison forces, liberating hundreds of enslaved dock laborers and securing the essential deep-water beachhead required for the overland march toward Sortengraahd.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Freezing spray whips across the bow of Cpt. T's refitted boat as the vessel cuts through the grey breakers of the northern gulf. Ahead, through the swirling sea fog, the black iron cliffs of Imnia North Port rise eighty feet above a jagged shingle beach. Two hundred paces out, a line of sharpened oak piles wrapped in barbed iron chains blocks the harbor channel, while muzzle flashes from two cliffside bronze culverins light up the mist with sudden thunder. Iron shot skips across the churning swells, sending plumes of cold brine thirty feet into the air as the order is given to hit the surf.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** The harbor channel is blocked by a spiked tidal chain boom, while an elevated 2-gun battery bombards approaching landing craft.

* **Tactical Objective:** Deploy Cpt. T's shallow-draft skiffs under the cover of W3, breach the iron chain boom (**DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** or **Thieves' Tools**), and secure a foothold on the shingle beach within 3 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The central boom cable snaps with a violent metallic twang, allowing the allied landing force to ground their keels on the gravel beachhead.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Imnia North Port was established two centuries ago as a whaling haven before imperial governors fortified its headlands. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The shingle beach is seeded with barbed caltrops; traversing the beach without clearing the lane deals **2d8 piercing damage** and halves movement speed. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Battery Commander Skane relies on line-of-sight signal flags; capturing the signal tower blinds his gunners to landing movements. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Shouting news of the total liberation of Braaken (**DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation)**) causes local conscript infantry to desert their posts. Discovery: Automatic after beachhead secured.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A cliffside cargo hoist tunnel allows small teams to bypass the main zigzag ascent path. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** In six hours, the imperial high tide will flood the lower beach, cutting off the landing craft if the cliff parapet is not captured. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The boom winch is locked with a three-dog ratchet; striking the release pawl with an iron maul drops the chain instantly. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Using **W1 (Tide-Walker Disc)** allows the party to sprint across the surface of the tidal swells, completely bypassing the barbed beach obstacles. Discovery: Natural synergy with W1.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked iron strongbox in the battery blockhouse holds 80 gp, two potions of water breathing, and the regional road atlas to Sortengraahd. Discovery: **DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Intercepted dispatches confirm that Verrick has established a fortified occult garrison in the capital of Sortengraahd, preparing mass defensive lines. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Beach Assault

### Area 1: The Barbed Shingle Flats (The Landing)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 200 ft. wide gravel beach swept by crashing waves and littered with iron anti-boat spikes.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 4 Coastal Marines (AC 16, HP 32 each) + 2 Heavy Crossbowmen (AC 14, HP 26 each) + beach caltrop hazard.
* **Tactical Objective:** Secure the high-tide line and clear the landing lane for following dwarven troop transports.

### Area 2: The Cliffside Gun Battery (The Strongpoint)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. stone bastion perched sixty feet above the sea, mounting twin bronze culverins.
* **Encounter (Battery Defense):**
  * **Battery Commander Skane** (veteran, AC 17, HP 58, longsword +6, 1d8+3 slashing, commanding shout) + **4 Artillery Gunners** (AC 13, HP 22 each).
  * **The Cannons:** The culverins can be captured and traversed to bombard the retreating garrison barracks (**4d10 bludgeoning damage** per shot).

### Area 3: The Upper Harbor Stockade (The Beachhead Depot)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Fortified wooden perimeter containing timber warehouses, repair slips, and labor barracks.
* **Securing the Base (Gated Event):** Liberating two hundred enslaved dockwrights, establishing the forward logistics depot, and preparing the supply wagons for the march north.
