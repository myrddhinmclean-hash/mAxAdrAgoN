# Epilogue: The World After the Blood Moon

**Module Class:** Epilogue (DM-Facing World Panorama)
**Setting:** The harbors of Braaken, the silent mountain passes of the north, distant imperial scriptoriums, and the open swells of the Kladesh Ocean.
**Overview:** On the morning of Day 38 + 31, pale winter sunlight touches the high peaks of Glazhenge for the first time in two centuries. The celestial conjunction has shattered, the occupying legions have dispersed into the wilderness, and the mortal realms begin the long work of reconstruction. Across thousands of leagues, the world continues to turn, carrying unresolved grievances, ancient covenants, and living mysteries that extend far beyond the final battlefield.

---

## 1. The Harbor of Braaken and the Royal Question

The timber cranes of Braaken are already turning by mid-morning, lifting charred beams from the harbor basin under the watchful eyes of dwarven engineers in boiled leather. Along the grand stone wharves, the flags of the free fire brigades snap in the salt breeze beside azure standards bearing the golden dragon crest. In the lower city, the markets are crowded with fishermen repairing torn nets and merchants haggling over flour shipments arriving from the south.

Yet the seat of power remains quiet and unsettled. Inside the Citadel rotunda, the council chamber tables are covered in competing petitions from dwarven guildmasters, waveborn envoys, and elven nobles debating the nature of the crown. Prince Glori sits at the head of the long table beneath the star-iron arches, listening in silence to the voices of those who demand a king and those who demand a free commonwealth, leaving the ultimate shape of the government unwritten.

Out in the center of the bay, beneath thirty fathoms of clear green water, the colossal stone form of the dragon golem rests upon its ancient submerged pedestal. Thick sea kelp and white barnacles have already begun to gather across its granite claws, while the strongbox of ITEM X embedded in its chest emits a faint, steady pulse of amber luminescence through the deep ocean trenches.

---

## 2. The Unseen Ledger and the Persistent Machinery

Three hundred leagues to the east, deep within the vaulted archives of an imperial administrative redoubt that never saw a single rebel banner, a lone junior clerk dips an iron quill into black gall ink. The fortress garrison above has received no dispatches from the capital in three weeks, yet the heavy bronze pneumatic tubes in the cellar wall continue to hiss with pressurized air.

Upon the central reading desk, bound in indestructible dark pigskin and stamped with the geometric eye, the **Tithe Ledger** turns its own vellum pages without human touch. Black script blooms autonomously across the parchment, recording timber shipments from the inland valleys, copper debts owed by distant hamlets, and the names of new children born along the frontier. The Handler is dead and the armies are broken, but the institutional machinery of the Empire continues its silent accounting, waiting for a new master to turn the key.

---

## 3. The Northern Ridge and the Unspoken Grudge

High in the frozen wind-gaps of the northern mountain range, four horsemen in dark fur cloaks ride single file along the narrow goat track that crosses into the eastern wastes. Their horses are lean, black-coated coursers whose breath plumes into the sub-zero air like smoke.

The lead rider carries a long black recurve bow across their saddle pommel, their face concealed behind a hood of wolf pelt. Slung across the pack saddle of the second mount, wrapped in coarse sacking, rests a locked iron coffer taken from the ruins of Sortengraahd. Reaching the crest of the pass, the riders pause for three silent heartbeats, looking back down toward the liberated valley with cold, unyielding eyes before spurring their mounts eastward into the pine forests, their grudge over W2 held in quiet reserve.

---

## 4. The Thawing Frontier and the Driftwood Shore

Along the foothills of the northern provinces, the great glacial ice shelves have begun to groan and calve, sending rivers of cold meltwater rushing through the scorched stone valleys. Free folk and mountain clans emerge from their cave shelters to gather timber and rebuild shattered stone homesteads beneath the clear blue sky.

Two hundred leagues away, where the Kladesh Ocean breaks against the grey sandbars of the western shoals, a waveborn foraging party discovers a heavy iron-banded minotaur horn half-buried in the tide drift. The horn is notched with seven naval kill marks, and nearby in the surf, shattered oak timbers from a heavy war galley roll in the foaming breakers, leaving the fate of Captain Mohdraan Boltstrucken open to the tides.

---

## 5. The Open Sea and the Horizon Ahead

Out on the deep azure swells of the Kladesh Ocean, well beyond the sight of land, Cpt. T's refitted flagship cuts through the rolling waves under full canvas. The vessel's hull is draped in the shimmering grey weave of **W3**, bending the sunlight around its wooden timbers so that from fifty paces away it appears as nothing more than a passing cloud shadow upon the water.

Dwarven star-iron plates along the waterline glint through the foam as the ship's bowsprit rises and falls against the steady ocean swell. Standing upon the stern castle beside the great brass helm, Cpt. T checks the bronze compass mounted to the binnacle, adjusts the mainsheet with a weathered hand, and sets the ship's heading toward the unmapped waters of the western horizon.
