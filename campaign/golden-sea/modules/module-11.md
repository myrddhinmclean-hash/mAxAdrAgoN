# The Hollow Deeps and the Concentric Sewer Labyrinth

**Level:** 4
**Setting:** The abandoned grain silos of the Hollow Deeps and the massive subterranean stone concentric sewer labyrinth beneath the city of Braaken.
**Main Threat:** Imperial sewer patrol squads, mutated subterranean predators, flooded sluice traps, and the corrupting presence of the Tithe Ledger.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 34, using the deed won at the tournament, the party unseals the Hollow Deeps granaries and descends into Braaken's ancient sewer system. Structured as four concentric granite rings surrounding a mysterious glowing central cistern, the labyrinth provides the sole unmonitored passage beneath the city's fortified walls. The party fights through imperial search details and sewer horrors to clear the outer rings, opening the submerged water gates for outside dwarven and waveborn assault forces. Within a bricked-up storage ring once maintained by Mother Ashfall, the party discovers her emergency cache, containing gold, rebel weapons, and the sinister, indestructible **Tithe Ledger**. From a dying rebel scout recovered in the tunnels, the party learns that imperial inquisitors have begun transferring political captives to the High Prison torture vaults, setting up the prison assault.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The smell of moldering grain and wet river silt hangs thick in the dark silence of the Hollow Deeps. Beneath the floorboards of the grand grain silo, an iron ring set into massive flagstones yields to the party's tournament key with a groaning screech. Cold air rushes up from the black shaft below, carrying the distant rush of subterranean waters and the rhythmic clinking of iron shovels. Looking down with a hooded lantern, the party sees the curve of the first great granite ring, where four imperial sappers in breastplates are methodically wedging iron portcullises into the stone archway to seal the under-city forever.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** Four imperial combat sappers and one garrison sergeant are attempting to drop an iron portcullis that will seal the outer sewer ring within 3 rounds.

* **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the sapper detail before the winch mechanism is spiked (**DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** or disable the winch with **DC 13 Thieves' Tools**).

* **Transition Trigger:** The winch lever is secured or the sappers flee into the second ring, leaving the entrance to the concentric labyrinth clear.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The concentric sewer rings were engineered five centuries ago by dwarven hydrologists using self-flushing siphon cisterns. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The masonry arch between the second and third rings is severely cracked; concussive force drops **3d6 bludgeoning rubble** across a 15 ft. zone. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The imperial sewer captain carries an alchemical lamp; shattering the glass flask blinds the patrol and ignites sewer gasses for **2d6 fire damage**. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Unlocking the southern sluice floods the imperial checkpoint in the upper channel, driving off twenty garrison reinforcements without combat. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A high dry pipe in the third ring leads directly upward into the exercise yard of Braaken High Prison. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The EoE inquisitors in High Prison have received orders to execute all high-value political prisoners before the solar eclipse. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** from the dying scout.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The sealed brick ring opens when the four elemental glyphs around the keystone are pressed in clockwise sequence: Earth, Water, Fire, Air. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** or **Investigation**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The central glowing chamber pulses with deep terrestrial abjuration magic, repelling planar fiends and aberrant creatures. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** Mother Ashfall's hidden room contains 60 gp, four masterwork dwarven war picks, and the **Tithe Ledger**. Discovery: Unsealing the bricked ring.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** An imperial manifest clerk held in the High Prison dungeon knows the full activation schedule for the bay dragon golem. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** from rebel captives.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Concentric Labyrinth

### Area 1: The Outer Drainage Ring (Ring 1)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 20 ft. wide arched masonry tunnel with a 5 ft. deep central water channel, smelling of damp limestone.
* **Encounter (Sapper Patrol):** 4 Imperial Sappers (AC 15, HP 18 each) + 1 Patrol Sergeant (AC 16, HP 28, multiattack, longsword +5 to hit).
* **Tactical Objective:** Secure the primary winch to keep the entrance corridor open for following rebel units.

### Area 2: The Flooded Lower Sluices (Ring 2 & 3)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Submerged stone walkways where waist-deep dark water flows toward the harbor siphons.
* **Hazard & Predator Clash:**
  * **Hazard:** Waist-deep water is difficult terrain.
  * **Combatants:** 1 Sewer Otyugh (AC 14, HP 68, tentacle grapple +6 to hit, bite +6) + 2 Giant Leeches (AC 12, HP 22 each).

### Area 3: Ashfall's Sealed Ring Cache (The Hidden Vault)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 25 ft. dry circular chamber behind a false brick partition in the third ring.
* **The Discoveries:**
  * **Rebel Armory:** Crated dwarven crossbows, rations, and 60 gp in coin.
  * **The Tithe Ledger (Ruling 26):** A heavy tome bound in cold blackened iron, its parchment pages covered in auto-writing dark script detailing life debts, tribute quotas, and occult payments across the Empire. The party discovers the ledger cannot be burned, cut, or damaged by ordinary or magical means. It is secured as a critical piece of intelligence for the broader war against EoE.

### Area 4: The Central Glowing Cistern (The Open Mystery)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. circular domed chamber where pure emerald luminescence rises from a crystal-clear subterranean reservoir.
* **The Unresolved Mystery:** The water here is pristine and sweet, radiating ancient elemental magic that hums in harmony with W1. Its origins and ultimate function remain completely unexplained, left as an open wonder beneath the occupied city.
