# The Dock Assault

**Level:** 1
**Setting:** FairWhether docks, the lower city's waterfront and pier district, including the inner pier, the outer pier, the dock master's office, and the dock tavern The Tidal Lantern
**Main Threat:** The Empire occupation, embodied by Kobold Captain Skritch and the last kobold garrison detail holding the inner pier
**Adventure Summary:** The dawn assault has just concluded and the party is consolidating the docks. The rebellion needs the inner pier clear, the two Empire longboats secured, and a courier flight staged for tomorrow. If Skritch holds the inner pier past noon, the Empire garrison sends a relief column and the assault fails.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The lower city smells of coal smoke, fish, and the sharp tang of lamp oil that has been on fire and then put out. The dawn assault has just ended, and the cobbles of the dock road are slick with a mix of puddle water and a dark thing nobody is naming yet. A line of freed rebellion prisoners is being walked toward the meeting house, and the freed prisoners are walking faster than the line can hold, and somewhere on the inner pier a kobold is still screaming, but not for long.

* **Active Obstacle:** Kobold Captain Skritch and the last garrison detail hold the inner pier. The detail is five kobolds, including the red armband conscript. Skritch has barricaded the pier office with oil drums and is refusing to surrender. The party has 3 rounds of in game time (about 6 real world minutes) to clear the pier office before the Empire garrison sends a runner.
* **Tactical Objective:** Breach the pier office, neutralize Skritch or drive him off the pier, secure the office, and signal the liaison that the inner pier is in rebellion hands. The runner deadline is 10 in game minutes (about 20 real world minutes) from the start of the session.
* **Transition Trigger:** The dock master's signal horn sounds once, long, from the inner pier office window. The horn is the signal. The Empire garrison hears it. The rebellion hears it. The session is now a clock.

## 2. Universal Hooks

The four hooks are rewritten from the engine template to fit the FairWhether campaign. None are rolled. All four are active in this session as facts the party can discover.

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Two Ships, Secured).** The Iron Promise and the Sable Wake are tied up at the outer pier, intact. Vellis has confirmed their names and rigging. The liaison will pay 75 gp to anyone who can bring both ships to the inner pier under their own sail before noon. Bounty equivalent: 75 gp for both ships delivered.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Office Prisoner).** A dock worker's apprentice is locked in the pier office, held hostage by Skritch as a bargaining chip. The apprentice is a halfling named Wren, and Wren has been in the office since the assault began. Distress call: Wren is alive and behind the oil drum barricade.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Strongbox).** A locked iron strongbox has been delivered to the dock master's office during the assault, addressed to the liaison, marked with a quartermaster's seal. The unsealing is the strongbox, and the contents are ITEM X. The liaison does not know what is in the box.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Outer Pier Sentry).** Two kobold sentries are guarding the two Empire longboats at the outer pier. The sentries have orders to scuttle the ships if the inner pier falls. The ambush is the party's, and the lead sentry is not the red armband conscript.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

Ten concrete facts about FairWhether, the dock assault, the Empire's movements, and the courier flight. Each is discoverable in play through the listed check or social action.

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The FairWhether dock district was built on a filled tidal flat in the second age of the dwarven calendar, and the fill is held in place by old stone pilings driven into the clay. The pilings rot slowly. The dock master's office is built on a stone foundation that was originally a tide watcher's tower. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)** or asking Cpt. T on the docks.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The inner pier office has a second floor loft that was added during the Empire occupation. The loft is held up by two rotten beams. The beams will collapse on a failed structural check, dropping the loft onto the office floor. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** when entering the office.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Skritch's Pack Lord's Snarl ability requires him to issue a verbal command. The command is a specific kobold battle cry. If the party can disrupt the command (a successful **DC 13 Charisma (Performance)** counter shout, a Silence spell, or a successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check to identify the cue), Pack Lord's Snarl is denied for the round. Sunlight Sensitivity is inherited from the kobold base. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** the first time Skritch opens his mouth to snarl, or by asking the conscript Pip before the assault.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** The red armband conscript, now named Pip, is on the inner pier detail. Pip is the same recurring NPC from Sessions 0.5 and 1. If the party addresses Pip by name and offers a way out, Pip will lower his spear and slip out through a back alley to the dock master's office. Pip is not in Skritch's barricade. Pip is on sentry at the office door, alone. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Insight)** at the office door, or by spotting the red armband pinned on the wrong shoulder.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** The pier office has a back door that opens onto a fisherman's ladder. The ladder descends to a low dock used for small craft. The low dock is the rendezvous for the courier flight. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** at the back of the office, or by following Pip if Pip slips out.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The Empire garrison sends a relief column at noon, no later than 4 in game hours from the start of the session. If the inner pier is not in rebellion hands by then, the column retakes the pier in a single assault. The column is 20 kobolds plus 4 humans in heavy armor, plus a war drum. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Insight)** when the dock master mentions a horn, or by asking Cpt. T.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The strongbox is locked with a dwarven puzzle lock, not a key lock. The lock has three rotating rings. The combination is set to the current day's date on the dwarven calendar. The dock master knows the current date. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** on the strongbox, or by asking the dock master.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The two Empire longboats, the Iron Promise and the Sable Wake, were requisitioned by the Empire from a merchant captain named Vellis. Vellis is a quiet supporter of the rebellion, and Vellis has been feeding information to the liaison for two months. Vellis has not been seen at the docks since the assault began. Discovery: asking Vellis's mooring contact, or **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** on the longboat names.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** The pier office has a loose floorboard under the desk. The floorboard covers a small leather satchel containing 40 gp, a list of Empire patrol rotations for the lower city, and a sealed letter from the Empire garrison commander to the Empire trading post that names Garruk as a recovered asset. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** in the office, or Pip points it out.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The strongbox's quartermaster's seal matches the rebellion's pattern. The seal is not the Empire's. The box was delivered by a courier who came in through the side gate during the assault, and the courier is now gone. The courier is the same person the liaison has been waiting for. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Investigation)** on the strongbox, or the liaison reveals this at the dock tavern.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

The four keyed areas are mapped to the session's real locations. The first two carry monster encounters per the engine's Area 1 to 2 template. The third is the dock master's office, the session's social and puzzle hub. The fourth is the dock tavern, the rewards and handoff scene.

### Area 1: The Inner Pier (Entry Point)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. by 40 ft. wooden pier, the largest of three piers at FairWhether, with the pier office at the south end, three Cpt. T boats tied up at the inner berths, and two Empire longboats visible at the outer pier 200 ft. north. Lighting: bright morning sun, with the shadows of the pier office and a stack of oil drums cutting across the planks. Smell: salt, fish, coal smoke, lamp oil, the sharp tang of the dawn assault's aftermath. Sound: the slap of water, the creak of mooring lines, the muffled argument inside the pier office, the distant horn of a fishing boat.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The stack of oil drums at the office door is Skritch's barricade. The drums are full. A critical hit on a melee attack against the drums ignites the oil and starts a fire that spreads to the office in 2 rounds. (2) The three Cpt. T boats are tied up at the inner berths. The mooring lines can be cut as a combat action, releasing the boats into the current.
* **Encounter:** **Kobold Captain Skritch** (AC 13, HP 22, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: shortsword, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d6+3 piercing damage; Special Ability: Pack Lord's Snarl, recharge 5-6, target one creature within 30 ft., DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or Frightened until the end of Skritch's next turn; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight) plus **five kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; or sling, +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 bludgeoning damage; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight).
* **Investigative DCs:**
  * **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception):** the loft beams are sagging under the stored fishing nets.
  * **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation):** the strongbox on the desk matches the rebellion's quartermaster pattern, not the Empire's.
* **Area Loot:** 40 gp from the loose floorboard (Secret 9), plus the Empire patrol rotation list (worth 0 gp, a campaign thread that pays out at Session 4), plus the sealed letter naming Garruk as a recovered asset (worth 0 gp, a Session 4 thread).

### Area 2: The Outer Pier (Hazard Chamber)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. by 30 ft. wooden pier 200 ft. north of the inner pier, with the Iron Promise tied up at the north berth and the Sable Wake at the south berth. Lighting: bright morning sun. Smell: salt, tar, the wet rope of the longboat rigging. Sound: the creak of mooring lines, the lap of water, the low conversation of the two kobold sentries.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The Iron Promise's mooring line is tied to a cleat that has been cut halfway through. A hard pull will release the boat. (2) The Sable Wake's deck is awash in lamp oil, the result of a broken lantern the sentries have not cleaned up. A critical hit on a melee attack or a lit spark will ignite the oil and start a fire that takes the deck in 1 round.
* **Trap / Environmental Hazard (Lamp Oil on the Sable Wake's Deck):**
  * **Trigger:** A critical hit on a melee attack within 5 ft. of the lamp oil, a successful **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check to overturn a brazier, or a lit spark from a fire spell or torch.
  * **Detection:** **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** to notice the oil sheen on the deck before any trigger.
  * **Disarm:** **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** to soak the oil with sand from a bucket on the pier within 1 round.
  * **Failure Effect:** **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** for any creature on the Sable Wake's deck, taking **2d10 (11) fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success. The fire then takes the deck in 1 round and the rigging in 2 rounds, scuttling the ship.
* **Monsters / Sentinels:** **two kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight), guarding the two longboats under orders from Skritch to scuttle the ships if the inner pier falls.
* **Area Loot:** A coil of good rope worth 5 sp on the Iron Promise's bow, and a sealed letter from Vellis to the liaison in a waterproof pouch on the Sable Wake's helm. The letter is worth 0 gp but is a Session 3 thread.

### Area 3: The Dock Master's Office (Puzzle or Lore Hub)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 25 ft. by 20 ft. stone foundation with a wooden upper floor and a loft, lit by a single oil lamp on the desk. Lighting: dim, with the morning sun coming through the south window. Smell: lamp oil, paper, the cold stone of the tide watcher's tower foundation. Sound: the muffled argument from the inner pier, the lap of water against the pilings below, the creak of the loft.
* **Tactical Layout:** Two features. (1) The desk is heavy oak, 4 ft. tall, providing 3/4 cover. (2) The loft access is a wooden ladder at the north wall, providing a 10 ft. high vantage for a single creature but only 1/2 cover from below.
* **Interactive Puzzle / Obstacle (The Strongbox):**
  * **The Mechanism:** The strongbox is locked with a dwarven puzzle lock of three rotating rings. Each ring has 12 positions. The combination is the current date on the dwarven calendar, three numbers. The dock master knows the date.
  * **The Solution:** Three workable paths. (a) The party can ask the dock master for the combination, requiring a **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion)** check (the dock master gives it freely on a successful check, and on a failed check the dock master demands to know who sent the box first). (b) The party can pick the lock with thieves' tools, requiring a **DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check. (c) The party can break the lock with force, requiring a **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** check and destroying the lock. The contents are not removed by breaking the lock; the box is opened.
  * **Failure Consequence:** If the party picks or breaks the lock unsuccessfully, the box's contents are not visible. If the party breaks the lock and the strongbox is a forged rebellion seal, the contents are still boxed and the party is no closer to ITEM X. ITEM X is not defined in this session per the wave brief. The contents are described as a wrapped bundle of cloth, heavy for its size, that does not open further without a key the courier did not provide.
* **Encounter (Social):** The dock master, a dwarf named Halga, sits at the desk. Halga is not a combatant. Halga is the rebellion's dock coordinator and the person who will run the courier flight tomorrow. Halga can be persuaded, intimidated, or bargained with. Halga's primary concern is the relief column deadline.
* **Area Loot:** The strongbox (ITEM X, not opened, see puzzle), the loose floorboard satchel (40 gp + patrol rotation list + Garruk letter, see Area 1), and a small notebook of dock schedules worth 5 gp to the rebellion.

### Area 4: The Tidal Lantern, Dock Tavern (The Boss Sanctum)

* **Dimensions and Environment:** 40 ft. by 30 ft. dock tavern with a long bar, a back room, and a small dock side terrace overlooking the inner pier. Lighting: dim, with lanterns on the bar and the morning sun on the terrace. Smell: ale, fried fish, salt, the coal smoke of the kitchen. Sound: the low hum of dockworkers and rebellion soldiers on their first drink of the morning, the clink of cups, the lap of water under the terrace.
* **Arena Features:** One active hazard on Initiative Count 20. The terrace railing is rotten on the south side. A creature leaning on the railing risks falling 10 ft. into the harbor, taking **2d10 (11) bludgeoning damage** on a failed **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw**. Two cover positions: (1) the long bar, providing 3/4 cover, and (2) the back room doorway, providing full cover but locking the party in or out.
* **Boss Encounter:**
  * **Kobold Captain Skritch** (AC 13, HP 22, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: shortsword, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d6+3 piercing damage; Special Ability: Pack Lord's Snarl, recharge 5-6, target one creature within 30 ft., DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or Frightened until the end of Skritch's next turn; Pack Tactics: advantage on attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the target; Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in sunlight).
  * Minions: **two kobolds** (AC 12, HP 5, Speed 30 ft.; Attack: dagger, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d4+2 piercing damage; Pack Tactics; Sunlight Sensitivity).
* **Morale / Tactics:** Skritch is in this room only if he was driven off the inner pier and fled to the tavern instead of off the docks. Skritch is drunk and reckless if present. Skritch surrenders if reduced to 5 or fewer HP and his kobold minions are down. The two kobold minions rout immediately if Skritch surrenders or flees. The liaison does not fight unless attacked. If Skritch is not present, this room is the rewards and revelry scene, the handoff of the Multipass, and the courier flight briefing. The party can always end the session here.

Skritch flees at less than 20% HP (5 or fewer HP) if not cornered in the tavern. Skritch surrenders if reduced to 5 or fewer HP and his kobold minions are down. Skritch uses Pack Lord's Snarl on a cluster of three or more party members to scatter the formation. Skritch's Pack Lord's Snarl is denied for the round if the party disrupts the verbal command (Secret 3).

## 6. Encounter Math Breakdown

The session fits inside a 1,200 XP daily budget for four level 1 characters. The day has two guard duty encounters, a puzzle and social encounter, and an optional backup encounter at the dock tavern.

* **Encounter 1 (Area 1, Inner Pier):** 1 Kobold Captain Skritch (450 XP) + 5 kobolds (5 × 25 = 125 XP). Adjusted XP with encounter multiplier x2 for mixed foes = 575 × 2 = 1,150 XP (Hard). Skritch flees at 5 or fewer HP and the kobolds rout if Skritch flees, dropping the effective encounter XP to roughly 700 XP (Hard, lower edge).
* **Encounter 2 (Area 2, Outer Pier):** 2 kobolds (2 × 25 = 50 XP). Adjusted XP with encounter multiplier x1 for few foes = 50 XP (Easy). The kobold sentries may surrender on a successful Charisma (Persuasion) check, dropping the encounter to 0 XP.
* **Encounter 3 (Area 3, Dock Master's Office):** 0 creatures. Social and puzzle encounter. No XP.
* **Encounter 4 (Area 4, Tidal Lantern, conditional):** 1 Kobold Captain Skritch (450 XP) + 2 kobolds (50 XP). Adjusted XP with encounter multiplier x2 for mixed foes = 500 × 2 = 1,000 XP (Hard). This is a backup encounter and only triggers if Skritch was driven off the inner pier and fled to the tavern. Skritch surrenders if reduced to 5 or fewer HP and minions are down, dropping the effective encounter to roughly 350 XP (Medium).
* **Day Total (both required encounters to combat, no backup):** 1,150 + 50 = 1,200 XP (at the budget ceiling). Bend: the day total is exactly at the 1,200 XP budget with no slack. The session is balanced for a level 1 party that takes both encounters to combat. The party has multiple paths to reduce the encounter XP, including Pip's defection, the sentries' surrender, and Skritch's flight. The realistic day total is closer to 750 to 900 XP, well inside budget.
* **Day Total (both required encounters plus Area 4 backup, worst case):** 1,150 + 50 + 1,000 = 2,200 XP (over the 1,200 XP budget by 1,000 XP). Bend: the backup encounter only triggers on a failed inner pier assault, Skritch flees the inner pier at 5 HP, and the backup is itself conditional on Skritch choosing the tavern over the docks. The realistic case is 1,200 XP, the worst case is reported here as a bend in the assumption log.

## 7. Concrete Rewards and Magic Items

* **Coin Hoard:** 40 gp from the pier office floorboard satchel (Area 1 loot), plus 5 gp from the dock schedules notebook (Area 3 loot), plus 5 sp from the rope on the Iron Promise (Area 2 loot). Total: 45 gp 5 sp.
* **Consumable Item:** Potion of Healing (Common, no attunement). The party receives 1 potion from Halga as thanks for securing the inner pier. Restores 2d4+2 hit points when drunk. The dock master calls it "the strongbox's first aid." The owner may replace with a different common potion at the gate.
* **Permanent Magic Item:** **The Multipass** (Uncommon, no attunement)
  * *Properties:* The Multipass is a set of three Empire travel documents and a small forgery kit, bundled in a leather wallet. The documents are well made forgeries of Empire military orders, naming the bearer as an Empire courier on official business. The forgery kit contains a small knife, three blank papers, two sticks of red sealing wax, a brass stamp of the Empire trading post seal, and a small pot of ink. The documents pass a **DC 10 Charisma (Deception)** check when presented at an Empire checkpoint, and a **DC 15 Charisma (Deception)** check when presented at a high security checkpoint. The forgery kit allows a user with proficiency in forgery kit to produce one additional document per long rest, requiring 1 hour of work and a successful **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check. The Multipass is the rebellion's main travel instrument for the courier flight. The liaison hands it to the party at the dock tavern.
