# Garegstown Landing and the Cavern Escape

**Level:** 2
**Setting:** The cliffside stone docks of Garegstown, the steep stone staircases, and the dark smuggling caverns carved into the cliff face above the harbor.
**Main Threat:** Empire search details landed from the *Iron Fortress*, the local dock security garrison, and the claustrophobic hazards of the unmapped cavern ascent.
**Adventure Summary:** Following the evasive maneuvers of Day 7, the fishing boat grounds itself upon the stone docks of Garegstown under morning fog. With longboats from the *Iron Fortress* landing armed marines on the lower quays, the party must navigate the occupied docks, rendezvous with the dwarf wagoneer Thorek, observe the cloaking properties of W3, and ascend through the cliff caverns to reach the highland toll road leading toward Paradiso.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Morning fog hangs thick across the water, torn by the whistle of retreating ballista bolts and the crackle of smoldering sailcloth. The fishing boat scrapes its keel against the lower stone ramp of Garegstown under covering fire, shuddering to a halt beneath two hundred feet of vertical granite. Out on the grey water, the tail of the naval engagement fades into the mist as three heavy longboats from the *Iron Fortress* break away from the warship's shadow, oars rhythmically slapping the swell as Empire marines row hard for the jetty. Behind the stone warehouses, iron boots are already ringing across the cobbles as the local garrison begins sealing the dock gates.

* **Active Obstacle:** Empire search parties are landing from the *Iron Fortress* within 4 rounds under the cover of the morning fog. The local dock garrison has closed the main gatehouse, forcing the party to cross the open quay toward the cliff staircase before the cordon tightens.

* **Tactical Objective:** Evade the initial dock sweep, establish contact with Thorek the wagoneer near the cavern mouth, and secure the cargo inside the covered wagon before the marines reach the stairs.

* **Transition Trigger:** A whistle sounds from the shadows beneath the cliff archway. A stout dwarf in a wide brimmed hat motions the party toward a covered wagon tucked into an alcove.

## 2. Universal Hooks

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Dock Manifest).** A harbor patrol officer carries a scroll of vessel arrival logs. Securing it reveals which berths are watched by EoE agents. Discovery: **DC 13 Sleight of Hand** or looting the patrol officer.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Stranded Crew).** Cpt. T must scuttle or conceal the fishing boat to prevent Empire forces from tracing its origin. The party can assist in laying a decoy towline.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Smugglers' Caverns).** The cavern network above the docks is an ancient dwarven quarry system, now used as a covert supply route to bypass Empire customs.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Harbor Patrol Cordon).** A squad of four Empire marines and four kobolds attempts to pin the party against the harbor wall before they reach the stairs.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Garegstown was built around vertical cliff quarries during the second dwarven age, with all surface roads terminating at the highland rim. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** A wooden crane platform midway up the cliff staircase is rotted and will collapse if overloaded by more than three armored figures. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The marine patrol leader is overly rigid in following garrison protocol; creating a diversion at the western fish market pulls half the search detail away from the stairs. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Dock laborers harbor deep resentment toward the Empire requisition officers; a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** check encourages them to block search parties with handcarts.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A drainage conduit behind the net menders' shed leads directly into the lower cavern cistern, bypassing the guarded stair landing. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** Longboats from the *Iron Fortress* are landing reinforcements every ten minutes; delay will result in the entire cliffside being blockaded. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The heavy iron gate barring the cavern entrance is secured by a three dial combination lock tuned to dwarven seasonal markers. Thorek knows the current setting. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** or asking Thorek.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Verrick, the EoE Handler, observes movements from the customs balcony, noting descriptions rather than joining combat. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A false floorboard in Thorek's wagon conceals 30 gp, dried trail rations, and the treated canvas square known as W3. Discovery: Thorek demonstrates the compartment to the party.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** Beyond the caverns lies the granite toll road to Paradiso, heavily patrolled by regional Empire border guards and opportunistic bandits. Discovery: **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** or consulting Thorek's road charts.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

### Area 1: The Cliffside Docks (Entry Point)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 120 ft. by 40 ft. stone quay at the base of the sheer cliff, crowded with fish barrels, wet rigging, and morning fog.
* **Tactical Layout:** Open cobbles with scattered cargo crates providing half cover. Stone staircase ascending the cliff face to the north.
* **Encounter (Dock Patrol Detail):**
  * **2 Empire marines** (AC 16 (chain mail, shield), HP 11 each, longsword +4 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing).
  * **4 kobold scouts** (AC 12, HP 5 each, shortbow +4 to hit, 1d4+2 piercing, Pack Tactics, Sunlight Sensitivity).
* **Investigative DCs:** **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** to spot the rot on the stair landing; **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** to locate the conduit bypass.

### Area 2: The Vertical Staircase (Hazard Chamber)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 5 ft. wide switchback stone staircase climbing 200 ft. up the cliff face, exposed to wind and coastal drizzle.
* **Environmental Hazard (The Rotting Crane Platform):**
  * **Trigger:** More than two creatures stepping onto the wooden landing platform at the 100 ft. mark.
  * **Detection:** **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** to spot cracked timber struts.
  * **Disarm:** Bypassing via rope climbing or spacing movement one by one.
  * **Failure Effect:** **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or fall 30 ft. onto the lower switchback, taking **2d10 (11) bludgeoning damage**.

### Area 3: The Cavern Gate Alcove (Puzzle Hub)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 40 ft. by 30 ft. sheltered alcove carved into the upper cliff, housing Thorek's covered wagon and the reinforced iron cavern gate.
* **Tactical Layout:** The wagon provides total cover. The iron gate controls access to the deep tunnel.
* **Interactive Obstacle (The Dwarven Gate Lock):**
  * **The Mechanism:** Three rotating iron dials marked with dwarven runes. Thorek dials the solution while the party covers the alcove entrance.
  * **Alternate Solution:** **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** or **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** to align the tumbler pins without the code.

### Area 4: The Smuggling Caverns (The Climax / Exit)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Winding subterranean tunnel network climbing gently through solid granite, lit by occasional dim oil sconces.
* **Tactical Encounter:** Navigating the dark passage with the wagon. Thorek drapes W3 over the wagon bed, demonstrating its cloaking effect as an Empire search patrol passes beneath an overhead ventilation shaft.
* **Encounter Math:** Evasion and stealth check (**DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth)** group check) to clear the upper exit without alerting the surface checkpoint.

  * *Drive:* Guide the rebellion couriers and cargo safely through the caverns to the Paradiso road. Has AC 12 (leather), HP 13.
* **Cpt. T (Captain Trident)** (water genasi, she/her, ally)
  * *Drive:* Conceal the fishing boat and provide a rear guard before departing by water. Has AC 13, HP 22.
