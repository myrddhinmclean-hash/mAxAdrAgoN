# The Braaken Prison Break and the Fall of the Prince

**Level:** 4
**Setting:** The granite bastions, iron-barred subterranean cell blocks, and torture vaults of Braaken High Prison, rising over the rocky cliffs above the harbor.
**Main Threat:** Imperial prison wardens, elite inquisitor squads, and the personal strike team of the EoE Handler.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 36, following a day of quiet preparation, the party uses the sewer conduits to breach the lower foundations of Braaken High Prison. Their primary mission is to extract high-value political captives before their scheduled executions. Deep within the maximum security oubliettes, they locate **Prisoner 1**, a former imperial manifest clerk who defected from EoE. The clerk delivers critical intelligence: ITEM X placed on the bay statue is an ignition catalyst designed to awaken an ancient subterranean dragon golem when the solar eclipse peaks today. As the party fights their way to the escape sluice with the clerk, an elite imperial cohort commanded by the EoE Handler ambushes their rearguard. Executing the treason warrant issued at the tournament, the inquisitors capture Prince Glorinath and seize **W1**, dragging him off to the Citadel while the party barely escapes with the clerk into the lower city.

## 1. Strong Start

> Iron torches hiss in the damp draft of the lower prison corridor, casting bloody crimson light across double rows of reinforced oak cell doors. The party emerges from the high drainage grate in the prison wash house, their boots splashing silently in the soapy runoff. Down the corridor, the heavy tread of hobnailed boots signals the approach of the execution detail, accompanied by the cold, metallic scrape of an inquisitor's flensing blade against iron bars. From the third cell on the left, a hoarse voice whispers through the feeding slit: 'If you bear the forge mark of Ashfall, break this lock before the warden brings the brand.'

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** An imperial execution squad (1 Inquisitor Captain and 3 Prison Guards) is moving down Cell Block B to eliminate high-value captives.

* **Tactical Objective:** Intercept and subdue the execution squad within 2 rounds before they execute the prisoners in cells 4 through 7.

* **Transition Trigger:** The Inquisitor Captain drops the heavy brass master key ring as he falls, allowing the party to unseal the cell block.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Braaken High Prison was originally constructed as an elven sea citadel before the Empire reinforced its foundations with black iron ore. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The iron grating over the central oubliette shaft is rusted through; stepping onto the center square requires a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or plummet 30 ft. into the tidal pit. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Warden Kray is obsessed with administrative protocol; presenting a forged transfer manifest (**DC 13 Charisma (Deception)**) stalls his guard response for two full rounds. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Releasing the dwarven conscript prisoners causes an immediate riot in Cell Block A, drawing off half the garrison reinforcements. Discovery: **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A laundry chute in the kitchen connects directly to the outer sea cliff drainage pipe, bypassing the main gatehouse. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The solar alignment begins at noon today; when the sun darkens, the dragon golem in the bay will awaken and begin destroying the harbor infrastructure. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** from Prisoner 1.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The master oubliette lock requires three counter-rotations of the central bronze tumbler before inserting the silver key. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Thieves' Tools**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Prisoner 1 explains that the geometric eye sigil represents the All-Seeing Censorate, the internal occult security wing of the Empire. Discovery: **DC 10 Intelligence (History)** from the clerk's testimony.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked iron strongbox beneath the warden's cot holds 75 gp and four vials of antitoxin. Discovery: **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The EoE Handler has concentrated three hundred imperial shock troopers inside the Citadel throne room to prepare for the solar climax. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Climax

### Area 1: Cell Block B (The Infiltration & Liberation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. by 20 ft. vaulted stone hall lined with eight iron-banded timber cell doors.
* **Combat Encounter:** 1 Inquisitor Captain (AC 15, HP 38, rapier +5 to hit, 1d8+3 piercing plus 2d6 psychic) + 3 Prison Guards (AC 16, HP 22 each).
* **Tactical Objective:** Secure the master key ring and unlock the cells holding thirty dissident citizens and rebel scouts.

### Area 2: The Deep Oubliettes & Prisoner 1 (The Revelation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 40 ft. circular subterranean pit chamber carved from raw basalt, smelling of sea brine and damp straw.
* **Meeting Prisoner 1 (The EoE Defector Clerk):**
  * The party extracts Master Vey, an emaciated clerk clutching an oiled canvas document pouch.
  * **The Exposition:** Vey reveals that ITEM X is the core matrix of the ancient dragon golem. When the solar event peaks at noon, the convergence of celestial light and elemental water will awaken the golem to crush the occupying fleet. However, the EoE Handler plans to commandeer the golem using an occult override ritual once the Empire navy is sunk.

### Area 3: The Sluice Gatehouse (The Climax & Arrest of the Prince)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 50 ft. by 30 ft. fortified gatehouse straddling the lower sea sluice.
* **The Ambush & Capture:**
  * As the party escorts Vey and the freed prisoners toward the escape chute, heavy iron portcullises slam down on both sides of the corridor.
  * **The EoE Strike Team:** The EoE Handler appears on the overhead balcony surrounded by 2 Inquisitor Masters (AC 16, HP 45 each) and 6 Imperial Heavy Infantry (AC 18, HP 32 each).
  * **The Prince's Sacrifice:** Prince Glorinath activates W1 to create a localized surge of water that wrenches open the outer drainage grate, shouting for the party to take the clerk and escape.
  * **The Arrest:** While the party and prisoners plunge down the drainage chute into the sea, imperial inquisitors pin Glorinath to the flagstones with weighted nets. The Handler seizes **W1 (the Tide-Walker Disc)** from Glorinath's chest, executing the treason warrant and dragging the prince away in chains to the Citadel.

  * *Presence:* Cold, calculating, clad in ceremonial dark robes stamped with the geometric eye; personally oversees Glorinath's capture and impounds W1.
