# The Golden Sea, Crossing to Garegstown

**Level:** 2
**Setting:** The Golden Sea, mid crossing, on the fishing boat as the Empire warship *Iron Fortress* heels over to intercept.
**Main Threat:** The Empire navy, in the form of Captain Mohdraan "Smite" Boltstrucken's warship and the boarding action that follows.
**Adventure Summary:** The party is two days out of FairWhether on the courier flight, and the Empire warship *Iron Fortress* has just appeared on the starboard bow, heeling over to intercept. The party must survive the boarding action, manage the environmental hazards of open water, and break the pursuit without losing the rebellion cargo.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The Golden Sea is the color of old iron, and the wind has been pushing the same low clouds for two days, and the clouds have not moved, and the boat has. A fishing boat is not made for this kind of sea. The hull creaks at every wave, and the rigging sings a low note that the crew has stopped hearing, and the only new sound is the Iron Fortress that just appeared on the starboard bow, and the Iron Fortress is heeling over to intercept, and the Iron Fortress is much bigger than the fishing boat.

* **Active Obstacle:** The *Iron Fortress* is distant and closing, not yet in engagement range. The party has time to take stations, prepare defenses, and ready the cargo for evasive maneuvering.

* **Tactical Objective:** Prepare the deck defense, manage sail speed, and brace for the incoming boarding attempt.

* **Transition Trigger:** Glory appears on the water between the vessels, walking on the surface using W1 to distract the approaching vessel's lookouts.

---

## 2. Universal Hooks

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Empire Signal).** The *Iron Fortress* uses a flashing signal mirror on its quarterdeck to coordinate naval patrols. Disabling the mirror prevents additional patrol vessels from joining the hunt.
2. **The Distress Call (rewritten as The Approaching Vessel).** The *Iron Fortress* cuts across the fishing boat's wake, forcing an immediate tactical choice between evasion, parley, or defense.
3. **The Unsealed Ruin (rewritten as The Warship's Hold).** The warship's hold contains confiscated supplies and naval patrol rotation orders.
4. **The Direct Ambush (rewritten as The Boarding Action).** Empire boarders deploy grappling nets once within range to lock the vessels together.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Golden Sea was named for the color of the water at sunset, when the iron-rich silt from northern rivers turns the surface copper. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The fishing boat's mainmast is stepped in a rotten block at the keel that will crack under sudden strain. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Boltstrucken is proud and can be baited into single combat on a **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion)** check. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** The kobold boarders are intimidated by Boltstrucken; neutralizing him shatters their morale. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A bilge hatch beneath the mainmast provides an emergency access point to the waterline. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The *Iron Fortress* is closing steadily; if not driven off or evaded, the fishing boat will be captured. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The signal mirror on the warship's quarterdeck can be disabled by unbolting the mount or covering it. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Boltstrucken carries a bronze trident divine focus that allows him to summon obscuring fog. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Religion)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A false partition in the warship's captain's quarters holds emergency funds and patrol charts. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** Empire naval commands across the Golden Sea answer to coordinated regional overseers. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations

### Area 1: The Fishing Boat (Entry Point)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 30 ft. by 15 ft. fishing vessel crowded with rebellion cargo, ropes, and crew.
* **Tactical Layout:** Rotten mainmast block (hazard) and bilge escape hatch.
* **Investigative DCs:** **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)** for mast integrity; **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** for bilge hatch.

### Area 2: The Golden Sea (Hazard Chamber)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Cold, choppy open water separating the two vessels.
* **Environmental Hazard (Drowning & Cold):** **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** on entering water, 2d10 (11) cold damage on failure.

### Area 3: The Iron Fortress's Deck (Tactical Hub)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. by 25 ft. warship deck with raised quarterdeck and forecastle.
* **Tactical Layout:** Signal mirror assembly and boarding tackle.
* **Encounter:** **Captain Mohdraan "Smite" Boltstrucken, minotaur captain** (AC 14, HP 45, greataxe +6, Charge, bronze trident) plus **three kobold boarders** (AC 12, HP 5 each, Pack Tactics).

### Area 4: Open Water Disengagement (The Climax)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Open sea shrouded in fog banks as the vessels disengage.
* **Encounter Math:** Driving off Boltstrucken or disabling the pursuit allows the fishing boat to slip away into the fog.
