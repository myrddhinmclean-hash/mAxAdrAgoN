# The Tournament of the Sundered Crown: The Archery Trials

**Level:** 3
**Setting:** The grand archery meadow outside Braaken's northern battlements, featuring thirty-yard target lanes, hay bale embankments, painted bullseye targets, and elevated judges' galleries.
**Main Threat:** Master imperial marksmen, sudden crosswinds, and undercover EoE target spotters rigging the official scoreboards.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 32, competition shifts to the Archery Trials of the Sundered Crown. As the party competes across three intense rounds of ranged target shooting, they notice a blatant scoring fraud: imperial archers missing the outer rings are systematically awarded Gold bullseyes by bribed pit spotters waving yellow signaling flags. By infiltrating the target berms, subduing the fraudulent spotters, and making clean precision shots under high wind conditions, the party carries the Ashfall banner to another victory, igniting vocal public outrage against the corrupt judges.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Morning fog rolls off the Braaken estuary, clinging in damp beads to the straw target bosses fifty yards down the shooting range. The archery meadow is ringed by thousands of restless citizens, their patience frayed after yesterday's exposed jousting cheats. At the center line, an imperial sniper releases a black-fletched shaft that buries itself six inches into the outer turf, well wide of the target face. From the pit behind the target, a yellow marker flag instantly rises, and the imperial herald shouts 'Nine points to the Gold for the garrison banner', drawing a furious storm of jeers and thrown cabbage stalks from the working-class benches.

* **Active Obstacle (Scheduled Event):** The party's archer must step to the line for Round 1 while spotters and scouts in the party investigate the pit behind the target berm to stop the fraudulent scoring before the round concludes.

* **Tactical Objective:** Score at least 25 points across three shooting volleys while catching the pit spotter red-handed (**DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth)** and **Wisdom (Perception)**).

* **Transition Trigger:** A loud altercation erupts at the southern pit as the party's rogue catches an EoE agent swapping out genuine target boards for pre-drilled bullseyes.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & Tactical Mechanics

### Area 1: The Shooting Line (The Official Range)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 100 ft. wide grass lawn facing a row of painted roundel targets mounted at 30, 50, and 70 yards.
* **The Verbatim Scoring Mechanics:**
  * Each contestant makes three ranged weapon attack rolls per round against target AC values, consulting the official scoring table:

| Roll Result | Target Zone | Points Awarded |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | White (Outer Ring) | 1 point |
| 6 to 10 | Blue (Outer Mid) | 3 points |
| 11 to 15 | Blue (Inner Mid) | 5 points |
| 16 to 20 | Red (Inner Ring) | 7 points |
| 21+ | Gold (Bullseye) | 9 points |

### Area 2: The Target Pit and Embankment (The Cheating Hub)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 6 ft. deep earthen trench behind the targets with timber shoring and signaling flag racks.
* **The Sabotage Infiltration Encounter:**
  * **Combatants:** 2 EoE Target Riggers (AC 13, HP 18 each) + 1 Rogue Infiltrator (AC 14, HP 24, shortsword +4 to hit, 1d6+2 piercing).
  * **Tactical Objective:** Subdue the riggers quietly without interrupting the shooting rounds, confiscating the false flag signaling book.

### Area 3: The Timber Grandstands (The Public Turning Point)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Crowded wooden bleachers packed with merchants, laborers, and armed city watch details.
* **Rousing the Spectators:**
  * When the party's rogue tosses the false signaling flags onto the judges' table, the party makes a **DC 12 Charisma (Performance)** or **Persuasion** check to rouse the crowd, forcing the judges to award the Ashfall team full legitimate points.

## 5. NPC Roster

* **Lieutenant Kren** (human, he/him, imperial marksman)
  * *Appearance and Manner:* Cold, meticulous, clad in oiled brown leather. Relies on his rigged spotters while pretending to be an unmatched prodigy. Has AC 15, HP 30.
* **Elowen Windrider** (elf, she/her, independent archer from the southern vales)
  * *Drive:* Compete with honor; becomes a staunch party ally after seeing the fair scoring restored. Has AC 14, HP 24.
* **Barek Stonehand** (dwarf, he/him, chief herald of the games)
  * *Drive:* Preserve tournament tradition; deeply ashamed when presented with incontrovertible proof of imperial cheating. Has AC 12, HP 18.
* **EoE Spotter Cell** (human/dwarf mix, covert operatives)
  * *Drive:* Signal false Golds to ensure the puppet faction stays atop the leaderboards.
