# The Inner HQ Gates of Glazhenge and the Blood-Moon Revelation

**Level:** 7
**Setting:** The cyclopean obsidian archways, blood-rune gatehouses, and freezing sanctum plazas of the Inner Citadel of Glazhenge.
**Main Threat:** Verrick's personal elite Censorate guard, massive automated blood-rune wards, and the ticking countdown toward the celestial conjunction.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 28, the allied liberation army assaults the Inner Citadel HQ Gates of Glazhenge. Guarded by the final fanatical remnants of the imperial Censorate and bolstered by blood-magic runes, the inner gates represent the last barrier before the Vampire King's mountain throne. The party leads the breach: disabling pulsating obsidian rune towers, engaging Verrick's personal bodyguard, and defeating Verrick in single combat within the gatehouse vestibule. In the commander's inner sanctum, the party uncovers the grand strategic timetable: in exactly forty-eight hours, on **Day 38 + 30**, the **Blood-Moon Conjunction** will peak, enabling the Vampire King to sacrifice ten thousand captive warriors to permanently extinguish the sun over the northern realms.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Blood-red runes carved ten feet high pulse with sickly light across the colossal obsidian doors of the Inner Citadel Gatehouse. Through the howling blizzard, forty paces ahead, Verrick stands atop the fortified gantry, clad in spiked full plate stamped with the geometric eye, surrounded by four Elite Inquisitor Wardens chanting in dark unison. Behind the iron-studded gates, the mountain itself groans as subterranean ritual engines grind into motion, while a massive magical ward of swirling crimson energy repels the dwarven mortar shells, detonating them harmlessly in mid-air.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** The Inner Citadel Gate is shielded by a Blood-Rune Ward powered by 3 Obsidian Pylons and defended by Verrick and 4 Inquisitor Wardens.

* **Tactical Objective:** Destroy the three obsidian pylons (**DC 16 Strength (Athletics)** or **Arcana**), break the blood-ward, and breach the inner gatehouse within 4 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The final pylon shatters in a blinding explosion of necrotic glass, dropping the barrier and allowing the party to storm the gatehouse vestibule.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Inner Citadel was carved into the core basalt of the mountain to tap into the subterranean necrotic ley lines. Discovery: **DC 12 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The blood-rune pylons emit pulses of necrotic energy; standing within 10 ft. of an active pylon deals **4d10 necrotic damage** per round (DC 16 Con save halves). Discovery: **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Verrick's blood-plate armor is vulnerable to radiant energy; hitting him with **W2** or divine smites disables his damage resistance for one round. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Presenting the head of the gatehouse warden causes the remaining non-vampiric conscripts inside to open the inner portcullis. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A thermal drainage flume beneath the gatehouse allows two characters to flank Verrick's command platform. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event — The Strategic Deadline):** The celestial alignment of the Blood-Moon peaks on **Day 38 + 30** at midnight; if the ritual is not halted, the northern realms will fall under permanent necrotic eclipse. Discovery: **DC 12 Intelligence (History)** or captured star charts.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The inner gate lock mechanism requires simultaneously depressing the three star-iron keys captured from the pylons. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The fourth ancient relic, **W4 (the Dawn-Forged Scepter)**, is enshrined in the Vampire King's inner sanctum as the ritual focal point. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked mithral safe in Verrick's command tent holds 250 gp in royal trade bullion and two scrolls of banishment. Discovery: **DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Beyond the inner gate lies the Grand Obsidian Staircase leading directly up into the Vampire King's throne redoubt. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Inner Breach

### Area 1: The Obsidian Rune Plaza (The Outer Approach)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 120 ft. wide frozen stone terrace dominated by three 20 ft. tall pulsating obsidian blood-pylons.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 4 Inquisitor Wardens (AC 17, HP 65 each, necrotic blade +7, 2d6+4 slashing plus 2d6 necrotic) + 4 Blood Gargoyles (AC 15, HP 42 each).
* **Tactical Objective:** Shatter the pylons to bring down the central protective ward shielding the gatehouse doors.

### Area 2: The Gatehouse Vestibule (The Fall of Verrick)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. by 40 ft. vaulted stone hall flanked by arrow slits and iron drop grates.
* **Boss Confrontation (Gated Event):**
  * **Verrick (EoE High Marshal)** (CR 7, AC 18, HP 125, star-iron greatsword +8 to hit, 2d6+5 slashing plus 2d6 necrotic, multiattack 3, legendary resistances).
  * **The Resolution:** After an epic clash across the vestibule, Verrick is slain, ending his relentless pursuit and breaking the final administrative hierarchy of EoE in the northern province.

### Area 3: The Command Solar & The Stellar Map (The Deadline Revealed)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Octagonal war room lined with celestial globes, star charts, and glowing obsidian tablets.
* **The Grand Revelation (Ruling 34):**
  * The party discovers the master astronomical chart of the **Blood-Moon Conjunction**.
  * The chart reveals that in exactly forty-eight hours (**Day 38 + 30**), the moon will turn crimson over the northern peaks.
  * The Vampire King intends to consume the souls of the captured armies to fuel an eternal necrotic storm, cementing his absolute dominion over the continent.
  * Armed with the knowledge that **W4** is within the throne redoubt, the party prepares for the final ascent.
