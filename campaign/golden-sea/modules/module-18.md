# The High Castle of Sortengraahd and the Northern Escape

**Level:** 5
**Setting:** The soaring basalt vaults, obsidian war rooms, and sheer cliffside parapets of the High Castle of Sortengraahd, descending into a frozen river canyon.
**Main Threat:** The Evil Second Party (PVP 2), Verrick's blood-bound thralls, alchemical wildfire demolition traps, and an escape descent down two-hundred-foot icy cliffs.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 12, the party storms the inner sanctum of the High Castle of Sortengraahd. Within the grand obsidian archives, they engage Verrick's blood-bound thrall champions and secure the northern transit dossiers. Realizing his stronghold is lost, Verrick ignites the fortress wildfire stores and flees toward Bernax. As the towers ignite, the **Evil Second Party** intercepts the party on the crumbling cliffside parapets to seize the archives and settle the W2 grudge in a vicious direct duel (**PVP 2**). Driving back their evil rivals, the party executes a harrowing escape down the frozen cliffside drainage sluices into the river gorge below, securing the mountain route north.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Green alchemical fire licks at the black basalt arches of the High Castle's Great Archive, turning the air into a suffocating fog of sulfur and burning parchment. In the center of the hall, forty paces ahead, Verrick stands atop an iron gallery, holding a burning torch over the master archive chests while four massive thrall knights clad in spiked star-iron level their greatswords. With a cold sneer, Verrick tosses the torch into the wildfire siphon below, shouting 'Burn the northern maps, let the liberators freeze in the dark', before stepping backward into the shadows of the private turret lift.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** Four Blood-Bound Thrall Knights (veteran brutes) defend the central archive, while wildfire spreads across the floor at a rate of 10 ft. per round.

* **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the thrall knights, rescue the northern route dossiers from the burning iron chest (**DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** or **Strength (Athletics)**), and reach the western postern before the ceiling collapses in 4 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The master archive chest is wrenched free from the flames as the central dome caves in, forcing the party out onto the wind-whipped exterior cliff balcony.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The High Castle was engineered to be completely self-sufficient during five-year winter sieges, containing deep thermal spring siphons. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The burning marble floor is collapsing; stepping onto glowing fissures deals **4d10 fire damage** (DC 15 Dexterity save for half). Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Blood-bound thralls suffer disadvantage on attack rolls when exposed to the radiant energy of **W1** or sacred fire. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Severing the central blood-anchor crystal instantly neutralizes the thrall knights' supernatural regeneration. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A laundry chute behind the high solar leads directly to the mountain drainage flume. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** In exactly ten minutes, the subterranean wildfire magazines will detonate, leveling the entire upper castle plateau. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The drainage flume sluice gate can be deployed using an iron crowbar, creating a water-braked slide into the canyon snowdrifts. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Verrick's carriage is heading directly toward the industrial foundries of Bernax to oversee the Tithe Ledger's final processing. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked mithral coffer in the solar holds 140 gp in imperial trade bullion and a ring of feather falling. Discovery: **DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Recovered route charts show that the fortress of Bernax is ringed by heavy ironworks and automated defense turrets. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The PVP 2 Clash

### Area 1: The Burning Grand Archive (The Document Heist)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 90 ft. by 60 ft. vaulted basalt hall engulfed in spreading green wildfire, choked with thick smoke.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 4 Blood-Bound Thrall Knights (AC 16, HP 52 each, greatsword +6 to hit, 2d6+3 slashing plus 1d6 necrotic) + spreading wildfire hazard.
* **Tactical Objective:** Extract the northern route maps from the central iron chest before fire consumes the dais.

### Area 2: The High Parapet & The PVP 2 Duel (The Evil Second Party Confrontation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Narrow 20 ft. wide stone wallwalk perched two hundred feet above a sheer frozen canyon, swept by blizzard gales.
* **The PVP 2 Encounter (Ruling 39):**
  * **Combatants:** **The Evil Second Party** (4 Mirror Champions: Death Knight Adept AC 18 HP 65, Shadow Rogue AC 15 HP 50, Warlock of the Blood Moon AC 14 HP 48, Gloom Archer AC 15 HP 52).
  * **Tactical Dynamics:** The evil second party ambushes the party to seize the transit dossiers and avenge their tournament defeat over W2. The battle is a ruthless, lethal clash across the collapsing icy battlements.
  * **Descent Trigger:** After taking heavy damage, the rival rogue detonates a smoke bomb, and both parties make emergency descents down opposite canyon flumes into the blizzard.

### Area 3: The Frozen Gorge (The Canyon Breakout)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Deep snow-filled river canyon flanked by towering granite walls, swept by blizzard winds.
* **The Breakout:** The party regroups at the frozen river bed, repelling pursuing imperial cavalry before disappearing into the northern pine forests toward Bernax.
