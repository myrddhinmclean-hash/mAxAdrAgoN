# The Blood-Moon Conjunction and the Fall of the Vampire King

**Level:** 8
**Setting:** The floating obsidian platforms, roaring planar vortex, and star-iron throne of the Astral Pinnacle, suspended above the frozen mountain summits of Glazhenge.
**Main Threat:** The Vampire King (Ancient Dread Warlord), the Blood-Moon Planar Vortex, elite vampire spawn lords, and celestial necrotic radiation.
**Adventure Summary:** On the midnight of Day 38 + 30, the Blood-Moon Conjunction reaches its terrifying totality over the northern peaks. Standing upon the Astral Pinnacle, the ancient **Vampire King** initiates the grand blood-tithe ritual, channeling necrotic stellar energy through the floating obsidian altars to permanently plunge the continent into eternal winter night. Leading the vanguard of the united mortal host, the party ascends the floating platforms. Battling through elite vampire champions, the party seizes **W4 (the Dawn-Forged Scepter)** from the central altar. Uniting all Four Boons in a cataclysmic convergence of ancestral elemental power, the party breaks the celestial vortex and strikes down the Vampire King, shattering the last vestige of imperial darkness and winning everlasting freedom for the realms of the Golden Sea.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> The sky above the mountain peak has turned the color of freshly spilled arterial blood, dominated by a colossal crimson moon that fills half the celestial dome. Jagged islands of black obsidian float suspended in the freezing planar vortex, held aloft by crackling ribbons of dark purple lightning. At the center of the highest floating pinnacle, sixty paces ahead, the Vampire King stands before an altar of glowing star-iron, holding the golden radiance of W4 in his gauntleted hand as thousands of crimson souls swirl into the sky above him. As the party steps off the spectral ice bridge onto the pinnacle, the dread warlord turns, his pale eyes glowing with ancient malice as he raises his dread blade to strike down the mortal challengers.

* **Active Obstacle (Scheduled Event):** The Blood-Moon Conjunction Vortex deals **4d10 necrotic damage** (DC 16 Con save halves) every round to creatures outside the protective radiant aura of the Four Boons.

* **Tactical Objective:** Cross the floating obsidian platforms, engage the Vampire King, seize **W4 (the Dawn-Forged Scepter)**, and disrupt the central blood-altar within 4 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The party grasps W4, channeling its solar fire into W1, W2, and W3 to ignite a blinding pillar of golden dawn that severs the celestial vortex.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Astral Pinnacle was ancient before the founding of the first dwarven clans, serving as the terrestrial anchor for the northern planar gates. Discovery: **DC 12 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The floating obsidian platforms are slowly breaking apart; leaping across the void requires a **DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check or fall 100 ft. onto the lower ice shelf. Discovery: **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The Vampire King cannot regenerate hit points while exposed to the combined radiant aura of **W1** and **W4**. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Severing the four blood-tithe ley lines around the dais instantly disperses the Vampire King's legendary resistances. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A ribbon of stable gravity along the northern vortex allows a rogue to glide directly behind the central throne. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** At the fourth round, the Blood-Moon totality becomes permanent, rendering the Vampire King completely immune to all non-magical damage forever. Discovery: **DC 12 Intelligence (History)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** Uniting the Four Boons requires placing W1 (Water), W2 (Earth), W3 (Air), and W4 (Fire/Sun) into the four elemental sockets on the throne dais. Discovery: **DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (Ruling 29):** The ancestral boons resonate with an ancient harmony that transcends mortal history, driving back the darkness without revealing the inscrutable face of destiny. Discovery: Implicit in the convergence.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** An obsidian coffer beneath the throne holds 500 gp in ancient royal gems, a mantle of spell resistance, and the eternal charter of the free realms. Discovery: Automatic upon victory.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** With the Vampire King destroyed and the Empire shattered, the free peoples of the coast and mountains inherit the sacred duty of rebuilding the world. Discovery: The final campaign dawn.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Astral Climax

### Area 1: The Floating Obsidian Platforms (The Approach)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Four floating 30 ft. wide basalt islands suspended in a howling void of purple planar lightning.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 4 Vampire Spawn Lords (AC 16, HP 82 each, multiattack, claw +7, bite +7, necrotic drain) + planar wind hazard.
* **Tactical Objective:** Leap across the floating platforms under fire to reach the central throne pinnacle.

### Area 2: The Altar of the Blood-Moon (The Ultimate Boss Battle)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. circular obsidian plateau dominated by the towering Blood-Moon altar and the glowing golden relic **W4**.
* **The Grand Boss Encounter (Gated Event):**
  * **The Vampire King (Lord of the Crimson Eclipse)** (CR 10 Legendary Warlord, AC 18, HP 175, greatsword of draining +9 to hit, 2d6+5 slashing plus 3d6 necrotic, legendary actions, legendary resistances, planar vortex command).
  * **The Boon Convergence:** Seizing **W4 (the Dawn-Forged Scepter)** and uniting it with W1, W2, and W3 unleashes a 100 ft. burst of true solar daylight (**10d10 radiant damage** to all undead, blinding the warlord and shattering his immortality).

### Area 3: The Pinnacle of the Golden Dawn (The Campaign Finale)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** The high mountain summit as the blood moon shatters, replaced by the blazing golden light of a triumphant morning sun.
* **The Victory & Liberation:** The Vampire King turns to ash, the planar vortex collapses into shimmering auroras, and the united mortal armies on the glacier below raise a thunderous shout of liberation heard across the entire continent.
