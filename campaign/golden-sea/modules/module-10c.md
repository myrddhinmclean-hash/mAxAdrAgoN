# The Tournament of the Sundered Crown: The Gladiatorial Finale

**Level:** 3
**Setting:** The Grand Stone Colosseum of Braaken, a massive circular arena with sunken sand pits, spiked iron grates, elevated imperial boxes, and a central coronation dais.
**Main Threat:** The Dread Vanguard (elite EoE gladiators), illegal venom-coated armaments, and sudden riot outbreaks.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 33, the Sundered Crown tournament concludes with the four-on-four Gladiatorial Melee. Facing the Dread Vanguard, the party overcomes illegal poisoned flails and arena hazards to achieve a decisive, clean victory. Awarded 100 gp in gold and the title deed to the Hollow Deeps granary district, the party receives **W2, the Crown-Sunder Torc**. At the closing ceremony, as imperial heralds attempt to crown a compliant puppet governor, Prince Glorinath strides onto the coronation dais. Backed by the aroused populace, Glorinath rejects the imperial crown, throwing the ceremony into utter chaos. While dissident guild masters and tournament allies pledge their swords to liberate Braaken, the imperial governor issues an immediate warrant for Glorinath's arrest.

## 1. Strong Start

> War drums pound a deafening rhythm beneath the high granite arches of the Grand Colosseum. Fifteen thousand spectators lean over the stone parapets, their voices roaring like surf against a sea wall. At the center of the crimson-stained sand, four hulking figures in spiked iron half-plate step from the eastern gate, dragging heavy spiked flails that drip with dark, iridescent venom. Across the sand, on the imperial dais draped in purple velvet, the governor's herald lifts the golden puppet crown, waiting for the bloodletting to finish so the sham coronation can proceed.

* **Active Obstacle:** The Dread Vanguard (4 EoE gladiators) enter the sand wielding venom-coated flails. The central sand contains two hidden 10 ft. pit traps covered with canvas.

* **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the Dread Vanguard in open combat, avoiding the poisoned flail barbs, and secure the tournament championship.

* **Transition Trigger:** The final EoE gladiator falls to the sand, snapping his poisoned flail, as the chief herald raises the golden laurel and announces the Ashfall team as champions.

1. **The Bounty (rewritten as The Champion's Purse & Estate).** The grand championship awards 100 gp in mint imperial coin, the ancestral deed to the Hollow Deeps granaries, and the tournament champion's torc (**W2**).

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The Colosseum was built three hundred years ago over the main discharge cistern of the city's ancient stormwater drainage system. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** Two arena grates are rigged to open if more than 300 pounds rests upon them, dropping victims 10 ft. into spiked pits dealing **2d6 piercing damage**. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The vanguard leader, Ironjaw Thrum, relies heavily on sweeping arc attacks; stepping inside his guard negates his reach advantage. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Revealing the poisoned flails to the arena crowd turns the entire stadium into a deafening anti-imperial demonstration. Discovery: **DC 10 Charisma (Performance)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A drainage sluice beneath the champion's box connects directly to the Hollow Deeps cellars. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The imperial garrison has dispatched two heavy infantry cohorts to surround the arena gates before the crowd can disperse. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The Hollow Deeps deed contains a dwarven seal that matches the lock geometry of the upper sewer access gates. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** **W2, the Crown-Sunder Torc**, was forged during the first siege of Braaken, radiating abjuration magic that resists mental domination. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** An iron locker inside the imperial herald's box holds 50 gp and the signed execution list intended for the post-coronation purge. Discovery: **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The EoE Handler has ordered all political prisoners in Braaken High Prison moved to the deepest cells for immediate interrogation. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Climax

### Area 1: The Colosseum Sand (The Gladiatorial Melee)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 120 ft. diameter circular sand pit surrounded by a 15 ft. smooth stone wall topped with iron spikes.
* **The Melee Encounter:**
  * **Combatants:** **Ironjaw Thrum** (gladiator captain, AC 16, HP 45, spiked flail +6 to hit, 1d8+3 bludgeoning plus DC 12 Constitution save or take 1d6 poison) + **3 Dread Gladiators** (AC 14, HP 26 each).
  * **Tactical Objective:** Defeat the team in view of the judges without employing lethal atrocities, proving moral and martial superiority.

### Area 2: The Awards Dais & Coronation Platform (The Royal Confrontation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Raised marble platform 20 ft. wide draped in imperial banners and gold trim.
* **The Boon & Prize Award:**
  * The party is officially presented with the 100 gp purse, the parchment deed to the **Hollow Deeps**, and **W2, the Crown-Sunder Torc**.
* **The Collapsed Coronation Beat:**
  * As the governor attempts to place the laurel crown upon the puppet lord, Prince Glorinath steps forward into the arena glare, casting off his common mantle.
  * Glorinath proclaims the illegitimacy of the occupation. Bolstered by three days of exposed cheats, the arena erupts in rebellion.
  * The governor flees under heavy escort, shouting an immediate decree of high treason and an imperial warrant for Prince Glorinath's arrest.

### Area 3: The Northern Gatehouse & Under-Tunnels (The Escape)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Narrow fortified archway crowded with cheering citizens and fleeing officials.
* **Securing the Coalition:**
  * Guild leaders, veteran gladiators, and waveborn scouts rally around the party, agreeing to assemble at the Hollow Deeps to coordinate the liberation of Braaken.
