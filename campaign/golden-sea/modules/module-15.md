# Victory in Braaken and the Northern Threat

**Level:** 4
**Setting:** The sunlit harbors, celebratory grand plazas, royal council chambers, and bustling drydocks of the liberated city of Braaken.
**Main Threat:** Lingering imperial saboteurs, unexploded wildfire caches, and the looming, undefined shadow of the Vampire King.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38, the bells of Braaken ring out across a city freed from imperial occupation. In a solemn ceremony before the dwarven guilds and waveborn delegations, Prince Glori formally bestows **W1 (the Tide-Walker Disc)** upon the party, recognizing them as the true saviors of the realm. Down at the harbor, Cpt. T's shallow-draft boat is refitted with dwarven armor and oceanic enchantments. In the high council chamber, the party examines captured imperial archives, uncovering the cryptic shadow of the **Vampire King**, an ancient dread warlord gathering the broken remnants of the Empire in the northern province of Sortengraahd, alongside rumors of an ultimate fourth relic, **W4**. With the coast secured, the party prepares for the arduous overland march toward Imnia North Port, concluding the recorded campaign arc.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Azure banners bearing the golden dragon crest of Braaken flutter proudly from every battlement, catching the bright autumn sunshine. The streets of the lower city are carpeted in fresh cedar boughs and laurel wreaths, their fragrance mingling with roasting meats and festive ales from the open taverns. In the great sunlit plaza before the Citadel, Prince Glori stands upon the marble terrace before a gathering of thousands of cheering citizens. Turning to the party with deep reverence, the prince unclasps the shimmering green stone of the Tide-Walker Disc from his breastplate and places it into their hands, speaking words of eternal covenant between the throne and the champions of the sea.

* **Active Obstacle (Scheduled Event):** A rogue imperial demolitions officer disguised as a festival merchant has hidden a timer-delayed wildfire cask beneath the harbor festival stage.

* **Tactical Objective:** Detect and disarm the wildfire device (**DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** and **DC 13 Thieves' Tools**) before the noon victory toast without alarming the celebrating crowd.

* **Transition Trigger:** The brass timer clicks harmlessly into the water bucket, and the city wardens lead the cheers as the grand banquet begins.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The victory bells of Braaken were forged from the armor of the first imperial expedition that attempted to take the harbor two centuries ago. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The eastern drydock sluice is under heavy hydrostatic pressure; releasing the manual valve clears debris but requires a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** to avoid being knocked prone. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** The saboteur leader relies on alchemical flash pellets; anticipating his pouch draw gives advantage on saving throws against blindness. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Presenting the royal covenant decree to the harbor merchant league secures sixty days of free supplies for the northern march. Discovery: **DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A high mountain trail through the northern pass avoids the fortified imperial border forts, leading directly toward Imnia. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Nature)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** In the distant north, the Vampire King has begun mobilizing legions of blood-bound thralls to crush the liberated coastal provinces. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** from northern missives.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** Decoding the Handler's cipher ledger (**DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**) reveals that the fourth relic, **W4**, is entombed within the imperial capital's inner sanctum.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** **W1 (the Tide-Walker Disc)** allows its bearer to grant water walking and water breathing to their entire adventuring party for eight hours each day. Discovery: **DC 10 Intelligence (Arcana)** upon receiving the gift.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A sealed iron safe in the Citadel chart room contains 100 gp, a spyglass of true seeing, and the master map of Sortengraahd. Discovery: **DC 13 Investigation**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The surviving EoE handler Verrick has reached the northern stronghold, integrating his intelligence network with the Vampire King's occult guard. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Council

### Area 1: The Grand Citadel Plaza (The Bestowal Ceremony)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 150 ft. wide sunlit plaza paved in polished grey granite and surrounded by cheering citizen crowds.
* **The Formal Bestowal of W1:**
  * Prince Glori formally hands **W1 (Tide-Walker Disc)** to the party, finalizing the canonical handover (Glori wielded across Days 7 to 37; party receives on Day 38).
  * The party receives the royal covenant blessing, cementing the alliance between the crown, the dwarves, and the waveborn.

### Area 2: The Liberated Harbor Drydocks (The Vessel Refit)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Massive stone drydock basins where dwarven shipwrights and waveborn divers work in harmony.
* **Cpt. T's Refitted Craft:** The party inspects Cpt. T's vessel, now reinforced with dwarven star-iron keel plates, improved sail rigging, and twin forward ballista mounts.

### Area 3: The High War Council Chamber (The Northern Revelation)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Octagonal stone room dominated by an expansive tactical map of the northern continent.
* **The Revelation of the Vampire King (Ruling 30):**
  * Assembled leaders (Glori, Cpt. T, Captain Kael, Seluch) review intercepted imperial communiques.
  * The documents speak of a monstrous autocrat known only as the **Vampire King**, consolidating the remnants of the Empire beyond the northern mountains.
  * In the same dossier, references appear to the final ancient relic, **W4**, a power sought by both destiny and the occult Censorate.
  * Complying with Ruling 29, no cosmic motives or ultimate destinies are explained; the party resolves to march north toward Imnia North Port to strike at the heart of the darkness.
