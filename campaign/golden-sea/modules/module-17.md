# The Capital Street Battles of Sortengraahd

**Level:** 5
**Setting:** The cold cobblestone plazas, soaring basalt archways, and fortified garrison checkpoints of the provincial capital of Sortengraahd, shrouded in early autumn flurries.
**Main Threat:** Verrick's EoE Censorate enforcers, heavy iron barricades, street mortar nests, and rooftop sniper overwatch.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 38 + 10, after marching inland from Imnia, the party infiltrates the formidable provincial capital of Sortengraahd. Reorganized by Verrick into an oppressive armed bastion, the city is partitioned by iron gates and terrorized by inquisitor tribunals. Leading the urban resistance, the party battles through barricaded thoroughfares, destroying automated street traps and rallying suppressed dwarven guilds. During an intense crossing of the Grand Market Plaza, a concealed imperial sniper targeting the party is silently struck down by an arrow fired from a distant bell tower by their dream rivals. Reaching the base of the High Castle at dusk, the party breaks through the lower barbican, preparing to infiltrate Verrick's inner citadel.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Cold mountain winds howl through the high basalt colonnades of Sortengraahd, carrying the sharp scent of coal smoke and wet iron. The Grand Market Plaza is empty of merchants, partitioned by ten-foot spiked iron palisades stamped with the cold geometric eye of EoE. At the southern gatehouse, fifty paces ahead, an inquisitor captain clad in fluted black steel directs a squad of heavy pavisiers while an automated repeating ballista sweeps the cobbles with steel-tipped bolts. From the upper tenement roofs, brass alarm klaxons begin to wail, warning the district that the liberators of Braaken have arrived.

* **Active Obstacle (Gated Event):** An iron barricade defended by 1 Inquisitor Captain and 4 Heavy Pavisiers blocks the main avenue, supported by an automated repeating ballista.

* **Tactical Objective:** Storm the barricade, disable the ballista winch (**DC 14 Thieves' Tools** or **Strength (Athletics)**), and secure the market archway within 3 rounds.

* **Transition Trigger:** The ballista gear train shatters under a well-placed blow, sending the surviving pavisiers retreating toward the High Castle causeway.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** Sortengraahd was carved directly from the living basalt of the northern plateau to serve as an imperial mountain capital. Discovery: **DC 11 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The grand market aqueduct is fractured; applying concussive force drops **4d10 bludgeoning masonry** across a 25 ft. radius. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Inquisitor Captain Malis relies on an enchanted eye amulet for spell focus; targeting the amulet dispels his psychic warding. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Burning the censorate tax ledgers in the public square sparks a general strike among five hundred city laborers. Discovery: **DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A coal chute behind the grand foundry connects directly to the High Castle wine cellar. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (Scheduled Event):** At midnight, Verrick will execute sixty civilian hostages unless the lower city submits to martial curfews. Discovery: **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)**.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The automated ballista timing mechanism operates on a three-second wind-up cycle, allowing safe passage during the pause. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** Verrick's inner circle in the High Castle is protected by blood-bound thralls gifted by the Vampire King. Discovery: **DC 14 Intelligence (History)**.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** An iron chest in the precinct registry holds 110 gp and two scrolls of misty step. Discovery: **DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
10. **The Next Threat (Gated Event):** Documents in the guardhouse reveal the main road north to Bernax is heavily mined with wildfire barrels. Discovery: **DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & The Market Battle

### Area 1: The Grand Market Plaza & Barricade (The Street Clash)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 150 ft. by 100 ft. open square paved in black basalt, divided by iron barriers and automated ballistas.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 1 Inquisitor Captain (AC 17, HP 58) + 4 Heavy Pavisiers (AC 18, HP 32 each) + automated ballista hazard.
* **Tactical Objective:** Break the line before the mortar battery on the adjacent terrace zeroes in on the square.

### Area 2: The Cathedral Bell Tower (The Evil Second Party Intersection)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 80 ft. stone bell tower overlooking the central causeway.
* **The Gesture of Destiny & Self-Interest (Ruling 27, 29, 39):**
  * As the party crosses the open avenue, an imperial master sniper on the upper tower balcony takes aim at the party's wizard.
  * Before the trigger is pulled, a black-feathered arrow from the distant cathedral spire pierces the sniper's throat.
  * Looking up, the party glimpses the silhouetted archer of the evil second party lowering their black bow. The rival archer does not acknowledge the party, but turns immediately to rifle through the tower's arcane transit charts in ruthless self-interest before melting into the fog without a word, never aligning or cooperating with the party.

### Area 3: The Castle Barbican Gates (The Foothold)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Massive iron-studded gateway flanked by twin 40 ft. octagonal basalt bastions.
* **Combat Encounter (Gated Event):** 2 Censorate Shock Wardens (AC 16, HP 45 each) + 4 Garrison Crossbowmen (AC 14, HP 24 each). Smashed through to enter the High Castle.
