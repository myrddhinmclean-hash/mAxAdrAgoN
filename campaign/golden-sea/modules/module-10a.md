# The Tournament of the Sundered Crown: The Joust

**Level:** 3
**Setting:** The great tournament grounds outside Braaken's northern gate, surrounded by towering timber grandstands, royal silk pavilions, and twin packed-earth lists divided by painted timber tilt barriers.
**Main Threat:** Imperial garrison knights, unscrupulous mercenary jousters, and undercover EoE riders executing lethal sabotage on the field.
**Adventure Summary:** On Day 31, reeling from the assassination of Mother Ashfall, the party steps forward to claim her registered slot in the Tournament of the Sundered Crown. Amid cheering crowds and heavy imperial security, the party competes in the opening jousting trials. Between rounds, the party uncovers lethal sabotage: the EoE team is utilizing hollowed lances packed with lead slag and concealed iron shears. By exposing the tampered weaponry before the heralds and besting the imperial champions in the list, the party wins their first tournament victory and turns dissident noble factions in the stands toward open rebellion.

---

## 1. Strong Start

> Trumpets made of polished brass blast across the dry turf of the northern arena, their fanfare swallowed by the roar of ten thousand spectators packing the tiered pine stands. Banners bearing the imperial raptor flutter beside the vacant silk canopy where the puppet king is to be crowned at festival end. At the equipment pavilions, the herald calls the name of the Ashfall banner, and the crowd goes quiet, expecting an empty lane. When the party strides into the list bearing the black forge hammer of Mother Ashfall, the dwarven guilds stand to their feet in thunderous applause, while the dark-cloaked judges in the upper boxes exchange tense, furious whispers.

* **Active Obstacle:** The opening jousting bout pits the party's mounted champion against Sir Valen of the Iron Guard (veteran knight) in the central list, while party members in the paddock must inspect opposing lances for tampering before the third pass.

* **Tactical Objective:** Complete three consecutive jousting passes (**DC 13 Strength (Athletics)** or **Animal Handling**) while unmasking the illegal lance modifications before lethal injury occurs.

* **Transition Trigger:** On the second pass, an opposing lance splinters into jagged iron shards against the tilt barrier, revealing the illegal lead weighting within the core.

## 3. Ten Secrets and Clues

1. **City Origin (rewritten from Dungeon Origin).** The tournament arena was built over the ancient earthen ramparts where the first joint elven and dwarven moot assembled four centuries ago. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (History)**.
2. **Structural Hazard (rewritten from Structural Hazard).** The center section of the tilt barrier has a loose iron footing; striking the rail with a shield deals **2d6 bludgeoning damage** to adjacent riders. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.
3. **Boss Vulnerability (rewritten from Boss Vulnerability).** Sir Valen favors an aggressive left-handed couch; anticipating his tilt angle grants advantage on the unhorsing saving throw. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)**.
4. **Faction Disruption (rewritten from Faction Disruption).** Exposing the poisoned lead shards in the herald's pavilion causes the neutral guild judges to penalize the imperial team three tournament points. Discovery: **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)**.
5. **Secret Passage (rewritten from Secret Passage).** A drainage conduit beneath the royal pavilion allows quiet passage from the stables directly to the VIP viewing boxes. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
6. **Impending Doom (rewritten from Impending Doom).** The EoE Handler has ordered the final jousting round to end in an 'accidental' decapitation to eliminate the Ashfall team entirely. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** from stable whispers.
7. **Puzzle Solution (rewritten from Puzzle Solution).** The tilt weights can be rebalanced using dwarven counter-shims, restoring fair handling to a compromised lance. Discovery: **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)**.
8. **Guardian Lore (rewritten from Guardian Lore).** The elven nobles in the eastern boxes are quietly searching for any sign of Prince Glorinath, ready to declare for him if he shows strength. Discovery: **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)** while watching the stands.
9. **Hidden Vault (rewritten from Hidden Vault).** A locked iron chest under the herald's scoring table contains 30 gp, a silvered dagger, and the official tilt ledger. Discovery: **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)**.
10. **The Next Threat (rewritten from The Next Threat).** The imperial archery director is distributing enchanted target prisms for tomorrow's competition. Discovery: **DC 10 Wisdom (Perception)**.

## 4. Keyed Spatial Locations & Tactical Arenas

### Area 1: The Jousting List & Tilt Barrier (The Main Arena)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 200 ft. by 50 ft. compacted sand runway divided by a 5 ft. high whitewashed oak tilt rail, flanked by crowded spectator rails.
* **The Mounted Jousting Mechanics:**
  * Each pass requires an **Athletics** or **Animal Handling** check contested by the opponent's roll. Winning by 5 or more unhorses the opponent; winning by 1 to 4 scores a broken lance point (3 points needed for victory).
  * **Combatants:** 2 Imperial Champions (AC 18 (plate), HP 32 each, heavy lance +5 to hit, 1d12+3 piercing) + 1 EoE Saboteur Rider (AC 15, HP 26, tampered lance).

### Area 2: The Stables and Equipment Pavilion (Investigation & Hazard Hub)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** 60 ft. by 40 ft. canvas pavilion crowded with leather armor racks, oil barrels, and nervous warhorses.
* **The Sabotage Investigation Encounter:**
  * Inspecting the racks (**DC 13 Investigation**) reveals the EoE tampered lances.
  * **Encounter (Stable Cutthroats):** 3 Thugs (AC 12, HP 16 each) attacking with hidden iron clubs to protect the weapon cache.

### Area 3: The Guild Viewing Stands (Social Rallying Point)
* **Dimensions and Environment:** Three tiers of raised pine benches overlooking the list, shaded by guild pennants and guarded by city watchmen.
* **Rallying the Dissident Nobles:**
  * Presenting the tampered lance before Lord Alden and Guildmistress Vessa (**DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion)** or **Intimidation**) secures their secret backing for the liberation campaign, providing safe house contacts across the upper district.

## 5. NPC Roster

* **Sir Valen of the Iron Guard** (human, he/him, imperial jousting champion)
  * *Appearance and Manner:* Arrogant, clad in mirror-polished steel plate with gold trim. Fights ruthlessly but is unaware his squires are EoE plants. Has AC 18, HP 32.
* **Lord Alden** (elf, he/him, elder noble of Braaken)
  * *Drive:* Seek an honorable alternative to imperial puppet rule; deeply moved by the Ashfall banner. Has AC 11, HP 14.
* **Guildmistress Vessa** (dwarf, she/her, head of the Braaken Masonry Guild)
  * *Drive:* Avenge Mother Ashfall and ensure dwarven rights are defended on the tournament floor. Has AC 14, HP 28.
* **Malakor (EoE Paddock Master)** (human, he/him, covert logistics saboteur)
  * *Drive:* Ensure the imperial puppet team sweeps the matches by any lethal means necessary. Has AC 13, HP 22.
