* **Hit Points:** 115 (11d10 + 55)
* **Speed:** 30 ft.
* **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 9 (-1) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
