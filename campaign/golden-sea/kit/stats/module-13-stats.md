## Area 2: The Sunken Dragon Monument & Bay Waters (Module Lines 75–88)
* **Encounter Type:** Colossal construct awakening / Solar climax.
* **Evasion / Parley Viability:** Critical. The Dragon Golem is an environmental force of destruction; direct damage will not destroy it until the solar eclipse alignment is broken.
* **Ruling 42a Tone Guard:** Mandatory survival note. The awakening causes massive tidal surges. Characters standing in the splash zone suffer 3d6 bludgeoning damage without cover.

### Solar Dragon Golem (Awakening Construct)
*Huge construct, unaligned*
* **Module Source:** `module-13.md` L80 ("CR 6, AC 17, HP 95, slam +7 to hit, 2d10+4 bludgeoning plus 2d6 radiant, solar breath recharge 5-6").
* **Armor Class:** 17 (natural stone and star-iron plating)
* **Hit Points:** 95 (10d12 + 30)
* **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 40 ft.
* **STR:** 19 (+4) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
* **Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
* **Traits:**
  * *Solar Resonance:* While the eclipse alignment persists, regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn.
* **Actions:**
  * *Multiattack:* Makes two slam attacks.
  * *Slam:* Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) radiant damage.
  * *Solar Beam Breath (Recharge 5–6):* Exhales concentrated radiant energy in a 60 ft. line that is 5 ft. wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
* **Combat Behavior:** Sweeps the bay; targets structures and mooring chains rather than individual skirmishers.
