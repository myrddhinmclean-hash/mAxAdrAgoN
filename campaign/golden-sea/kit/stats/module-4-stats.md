## Area 3: Warship Boarding Clash (Module Lines 75–88)
* **Encounter Type:** Asymmetric naval assault / Pursuit survival.
* **Evasion / Parley Viability:** Moderate via tactical disengagement. Sinking the boarding cutter allows escape under fog.
* **Ruling 42a Tone Guard:** Captain Boltstrucken is an overpowering adversary for Level 2 characters. The party is intended to repel boarders and disengage, not duel Boltstrucken to the death.

### Captain Mohdraan 'Smite' Boltstrucken (Pursuit Avatar)
*Large monstrosity (minotaur), lawful evil*
* **Module Source:** `module-4.md` L20 ("Captain Mohdraan 'Smite' Boltstrucken"), L86 (`Captain Mohdraan "Smite" Boltstrucken, minotaur captain`).
* **Armor Class:** 14 (studded leather)
* **Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)
* **Speed:** 40 ft.
* **STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 11 (+0) | **CON:** 15 (+2) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 12 (+1)
* **Skills:** Athletics +6, Intimidation +3
* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
* **Traits:**
  * *Charge:* If moving at least 10 ft. straight toward a target and hitting with a gore attack on the same turn, target takes an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage.
  * *Labyrinthine Recall:* Can perfectly recall any path it has traveled.
* **Actions:**
  * *Greataxe:* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d12 + 4) slashing damage.
  * *Gore:* Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.
  * *Thunderous Rebuke (1/Day):* When struck in melee, releases a blast of thunder. Attacker must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) thunder damage and be pushed 10 ft.
* **Combat Behavior:** Bellows orders; leads boarding grapple; withdraws if his longboat lines are cut.
