## Why the text is not stored here

Pulling six documents into files costs a read of each, and the module work they serve is parked at G10. Paying that now buys nothing until G10 starts. The index costs nothing and the documents are one call away when they are actually needed.

Reverse this the moment G10 activates — at that point the descriptions become working material and should live beside the modules rather than in Drive.
