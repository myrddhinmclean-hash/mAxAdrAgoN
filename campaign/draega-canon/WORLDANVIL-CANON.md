## THE WORLD

Draega spins as a solitary planet around a bright yellow sun. Bright blue and green, beset by 12 continents, vast oceans, narrow seas, and huge tracts of arctic landmass covered entirely in ice. Home to all the races of the multiverse and some extraplanar beings. The yearly cycle runs 360 days, 12 months, 5 weeks per month, 6-day weeks, 25-hour days.

Two moons circle each other in orbit around it. **Phoegrin** ("fo-grin"), the red moon, is thought to bring the winter, signifies the ending of all cycles, and is known as the bringer of death. **Aqgui** ("ah-kee"), the blue moon, is thought to be made entirely of water, brings the beginning of cycles, and is known as the bringer of life. When night falls the two moons create a strange mix of red and blue light. Their eclipses bathe the planet in either a red or blue glow depending on which is in the fore. Both moons are always full; they seem to glow with their own light rather than reflecting the sun.

In the eyes of the multiverse Draega is a melting pot — somewhat of a backwater in its own corner of the universe, big enough to house many races and to give them room to flourish. All the gods of the universe have followers here, though not in the numbers other worlds see. Because of its distant location it is rarely visited by beings from other mortal worlds except by accident or divine intervention.

Current date shown on the world homepage: **16-6-7254**.

## CONTINENTS

### Azureland
Temperature ranges from tropic to temperate. Capital: **Paradise City** (note: the settlement article names the capital **Paradiso** — an unresolved conflict in the source). Mostly forests and jungles, very fertile. Several mountain ranges along the north edge get very cold in winter. Trades with the rest of the world in everything from food to wares. Known for many different kinds of settlements housing mostly forest races: dwarves, wild elves, centaur, animal hybrids, tabaxi, humans, halfling, gnomes, kenku, aarakocra, kobolds, simic hybrids, tortles.

### Frezho
Climate ranges tropic to temperate. Capital: **Braaken**. Mostly flat and marshy, fertile, easy farming; most lowlands are vulnerable to flooding. Trade goods are usually grains, forest vegetables and fruits. Inhabited by vedalken, yuan-ti, tabaxi, simic hybrids, animal hybrids, kalashtar, minotaur, loxodon, shifters, changelings, grung, goblins, wild elves, centaur, bugbears, firbolg, and the oceanic triton.

### Hemlar
Mostly plateaus, lowlands and high mountains. Farming is the primary trade good. Capital: **Laudnin**, the only large city on the continent. Temperatures range from the tropics to an arctic southern tip, but the land is less fertile than other continents. Home to minotaur, firbolg, wild elves, hill dwarves, bugbear, orc, ogre, goblin, human, halfling, half-elves, kobolds, loxodon, simic and animal hybrids, tabaxi, tortle, yuan-ti.

### Imnia
Forested, beset by a plague of undead. The undead exist mostly in the south, unable to overcome the volcano range that crosses the continent east to west. Fertile volcanic land to the north produces rare plants traded with Verna and South Moxia. Despite the constant threat to the south, many races live here: goliath, humans, dwarves, wild elves, goblins, kobolds, lizardfolk, gnomes, halflings, shifters, hobgoblins, orcs, bugbears, giants, dragonborn, aarakocra, kenku, tiefling. Capital: **Sortengraahd**. Holds the Church of Hope.

### North Moxia
The land of the northern elves. Capital **Garda** houses the royal house of Ellejhanikus. Several large cities and smaller settlements. Maintains an uneasy peace with South Moxia; trades with Verna and Frezho. The elves here are very distrusting of outsiders. Races permitted: high elves, tortle, loxodon, lizardfolk, halfling, gnomes, firbolg, changeling, aasimar, and the occasional tiefling.

### South Moxia
The high elves here are much more open to outsiders than their northern counterpart. Capital: **Normish**. Trades with Imnia and Hemla. Mostly forest and lowlands; produces rare plants the rest of the world wants. Tolerates a wider range of races: wild elves, half elves, genasi, shifters, changelings, kalashtar, loxodon, minotaur, tabaxi, simic and animal hybrids, dragonborn, lizardfolk, vedalken.

### Princia
Capital: **Glazhenge**. Known for mountain ranges, dense forests and jungles, and very fertile land. Many wars have been fought over it, most stemming from Hemla, and it remains sought by many. The fertility and temperature range have produced many large cities, each a trade center for its own good. Races: warforged, triton, vedalken, tiefling, kashgar, aasimar, human, half-elf, elves, goliath, gnomes, goth, genasi, firbolg, changeling, lizardfolk, dragonborn.

### Solith
Mostly temperate, the southern edge growing cold and somewhat arctic. **The most highly populated continent.** Capital: **Tohrgald** (spelled **Torgahld** in the settlement article — unresolved). Offers food, wares, beasts of burden, forged material, diplomacy, politics, religion, and acceptance. The many races of Draega have all found some way of integrating into its people.

### Vernax
Mostly barren, known for mountainous terrain and harsh climate. Mostly human, across a wide range of settlements. Seldom trades with any land other than North Moxia and sometimes Imnia; its rare earth elements and plentiful metal deposits are sought by the entire world. The human settlements often war with each other. Capital: **Yrlan** (ear-lan). Also home to dwarves, orcs, ogres, bugbears, goblins, hobgoblins, tortles.

### Zhork
Large mountains, rocky terrain, several volcanoes. Capital: **Gilna**. Very little farming because of the terrain. Houses mostly mountain races: dwarves, orcs, hobgoblins, bugbears, kobolds, goblins, humans, gnomes, gith, half-elves, minotaur, tieflings, aasimar, lizardfolk, warforged, vedalken, yuan-ti. Surrounding seas hold oceanic triton. Seldom trades, and when it does it is mostly rare earth materials and forged items. Contains **FairWhether**.

### The Greylands
Barren and frozen, housing only those most adept to extreme cold. **The land of the Ice Giants.** No government; the Ice Giants rule with no rivalry. Rare elements have been found beneath the ice, but the extreme environment makes retrieving them almost impossible. Few venture to this southern wasteland, and those that do are often beset by the giants or frozen solid.

### The Whitelands
Entirely covered in ice, to the north. Harsh climate producing almost no vegetation, mostly inhospitable. No governing body. Unlike the southern arctic it has a fair amount of wildlife, subsisting on hunting from the ocean or on each other. Few travel this far north, but there are a couple of settlements on the southern edges.

## OCEANS

### Kladesh Ocean
The largest ocean on Draega. Shares shores with Solith, Azureland, Princia, the Greylands and Frezho. **In the center is a large maelstrom of slowly swirling smoke and clouds that serves as a warning to wayward sailors.**

### The White Ocean (the White Sea)
Extends around the globe on the southern shore of the Whitelands. Borders Zhork, Azureland, Frezho, North Moxia and Verna. **It is the white caps of the tall waves that give it its name, rather than the continent it surrounds.** Few areas are fit for an ocean-going vessel: the many tides from the south collide into a vicious and unpredictable cacophony of danger. Because of this its depths extend to the deepest on Draega.

## SETTLEMENTS

### Bo'Cha — "The rock in a hard place"
Town, population 200, under Gargantua. **The fullest settlement article in the world.**

- **Demographics.** Mostly shipwreck victims from the Kladesh Ocean voyage. Some arrived by magic, either by accident or damnation. Almost every race on the planet, almost all of whom began their lives as sailors.
- **Government.** King Elanimous "Elan" Li'Zhulehgrin, Queen Yalenavenous "Yalina" Li'Zhulehgrin, and Prince Thinamikus "Thrime" Li'Zhulehgrin rule as a monarchy. *The source sentence "They came into power by" ends mid-thought — an open gap, not a fact.*
- **Defences.** 200-foot walls encircle the town with a single gated entrance, double guarded. A dock system allows 6 ships at once. 4 trebuchets and 10 ballistae surround the town atop the high walls.
- **Infrastructure.** The Royals commissioned a tunnel system connecting the keep, the walls, the two outposts outside town, and a secret entrance into the caves north of the town.
- **Geography.** A long beach surrounds the town on all sides, the southwestern side touching Gateway Bay. North and east of the beach is thick jungle leading into the Valley of Gargantua.

### Glazhenge
Large city, capital of Princia. As the capital of a warring nation it is fortified with thick walls several layers deep. Centuries of conflict with neighbouring Hemla have forced the "Glazen" into a military of efficiency and might. As a result the country has opened trade with nations on other borders, including Azureland, Solith and Frezho. While the city is maintained under strict military jurisdiction, it is also home to great libraries, museums, concert halls, and gladiatorial arenas.

### Torgahld
Large city, capital of Solith. A circular city acting as a center of commerce for the entire continent. The center is occupied by a large golden castle and its grounds, in which several tall spires can be seen. Roads from all corners of the country meet at a market surrounding the castle grounds, known as **Market Circle**, where the majority of shops and merchants gather. Further out are residences and smaller shops. The city walls stand 80 feet tall, a relic from wars long since fought. Outside the walls are miles of sprawling farmland extending to the surrounding hills.

### Braaken
Capital of Frezho. **Overthrown by the Empire in 7865 after a 6-year siege**, which led to the almost immediate surrender of the entire continent.

## LOCATIONS

### The Island of Despair
Bullywugs and grunds live on it and war with each other, in a murky swamp-like area in the middle of the island surrounded completely by sandbar and ocean. Numerous tales of secret magical devices buried in the swamp, lost there by previous adventurers.

### The Straight of Blood
A large sandbar on the southeastern side of the island, surrounded by red rocks renowned for bleeding. Rumors of the bleeding rock's medicinal qualities have circulated the island for centuries — it is said the rock can cure vampirism or lycanthropy, though nobody is sure which. Cursed individuals and the giants seek it to cure their own curse. Thousands of adventurers have tried to reach the strait hoping to recover the stone, only to be lost and never heard from again.

The strait has its own brand of carnivorous creatures, feeding not only on sea life trapped by the sandbar at low tide but on the many people and adventurers who have tried to make their way here. They are particularly vicious and seem to be somewhat sentient. The sandbar has many protruding rocks that tear holes in ships and boats, and consequently several shipwrecks lie in it. There is a series of caverns in this area as well.

### The Stone Giant Sanctuary
The stone giants — master stone masons and expert stone-throwers — keep to themselves in their own cavern. They are beset regularly by the fire giants and by several curious carnivorous creatures. They want only to be left alone in their tunnels to create art and worship their gods. The tunnels have been carved over thousands of years and carry many ornate and intricate carvings through the caves. Being solitary, they infrequently send emissaries to Bo'Cha for inspiration, and occasionally a renegade stone giant will join the community.

**Open thread:** the stone giant princess has been kidnapped by one of the villainous inhabitants of Bo'Cha. The shaman was tasked with retrieving her, and is on his way to the Crystal Cave where they have been tracked, having witnessed her imprisonment by way of a vision.

### The Crystal Cavern
Various formations have created a time-warp tunnel allowing creatures from the distant, distant past to travel to this time and exist on this very secluded island. The inhabitants have grown accustomed to these massive creatures and their dangers, but had to build a fortified city to protect themselves and to congregate the many outgoing expeditions into the island. Many adventurers landing here from shipwrecks travel into the Crystal Caves only to be eaten, or lost in the mazes of crystal formations buried within.

### The Fire Giant Fortress
The fire giants have created a fortified gate guarding a series of massive magma tunnels. They venture out frequently with siege weapons attempting to overthrow the main city or conquer the stone giants. **These fire giants are more vicious than their mainland counterparts.** They are led by the great king **Tohlrod**. They enslave massive dinosaurs as mounts and war monsters, and have built siege weapons from the massive trees and surrounding rock. Master metalsmiths who have perfected their art in seclusion. They war with the nearby stone giants, who rarely leave their caverns. Inside the tunnels they thrive with their slaves and their nobility, near a magma chamber that is always full.

## CHARACTERS

### Grimbah Gold
A paranoid, somewhat crazy, and brilliant deep gnome who escaped the Underdark and became a druid, out of the love of the sun and moon. Location: Solith. Has children.

**Role, owner ruling 2026-08-30: Grimbah is the omni-NPC — the one who saves the party.** He is not a local patron tied to one adventure. He recurs, he crosses modules and campaigns, and he is the hand on the scale when a party is about to be lost. Treat any appearance of him as load-bearing: he is the author's own instrument in the world, and using him casually as set dressing misreads what he is for.

*He is the patron in the published entry "Shell Valley" — the gnome druid who pays gold upfront, asks no questions, and gives four rules.*

## CONFLICTS AND GAPS WORTH KNOWING

These are unresolved *in the source*. Do not silently pick a side.

1. **Azureland's capital** is "Paradise City" in the continent article and "Paradiso" in the settlement article.
2. **Solith's capital** is "Tohrgald" in the continent article and "Torgahld" in the settlement article; the settlement article itself opens "Tohrgald is the capital of Solith."
3. **Bo'Cha's government** sentence stops at "They came into power by".
4. **Braaken's fall is dated 7865 A.G.**, which is later than every event on the history timeline (the latest is 7253) and later than the world's current date of 7254. Either the siege date or the world date is wrong, or 7865 belongs to a different reckoning.
