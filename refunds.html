Refunds

Everything sold here is a digital download, so there is nothing to post back. That does not mean you are stuck with something that does not work.

If it does not do what the page said it does, ask for your money back and you will get it. Fourteen days, no argument, no form to fill in. Email myrddhin.mclean@gmail.com with what you bought and roughly what went wrong. The second part is optional, but it is the only way the thing gets fixed for the next person.

If it has been longer than fourteen days and it is genuinely broken, ask anyway. A refund on a small tool costs me less than a person quietly deciding my software does not work.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through whoever processed the sale. How fast that lands is up to your bank rather than me, and it is usually a few days.
