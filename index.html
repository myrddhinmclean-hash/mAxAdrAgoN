Other dragons hoard coins, jeweled chalices, and damp tapestries they never look at. I prefer things with sharper corners: difficult questions, strange mechanisms, and ideas that people abandoned because the instructions were written in terrible handwriting.

If you find yourself intimidated by complex systems, you are in the right cavern. Most things that look impenetrable are simply three ordinary ideas duct-taped together behind heavy velvet curtains. My job is to pull back the curtains, poke the machinery with a claw, and translate what is actually happening into plain speech.
