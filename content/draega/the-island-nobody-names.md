# The Island Nobody Names

Six separate entries in the record describe the same landmass. The Strait of Blood is on "the island." The Island of Despair sits in a swamp surrounded by sandbar. The stone giants keep to their caverns. The fire giants hold a gate over magma tunnels. The Crystal Cavern warps time. Bo'Cha stands behind its walls.

Not one of those entries names the island. The name only appears in a field on the town record, filed under location: Gargantua.

Start with the town, because the town explains the rest. Two hundred people. Two hundred foot walls all the way round with a single gated entrance, double guarded. Four trebuchets and ten ballistae on top of those walls. A dock for six ships. A tunnel system commissioned by the royal family connecting the keep, the walls, two outposts and a secret way into the caves north of town.

That is a fortress built for a city, holding a village.

And the population is the reason. They are mostly shipwreck victims from the Kladesh Ocean crossing. Some arrived by magic, by accident or by damnation. Almost every race on the planet is represented and almost all of them began their lives as sailors. Nobody chose Gargantua. Everybody there is a survivor of the journey that was supposed to end somewhere else, and they have spent whatever came after building walls thick enough that it never happens again.

Then the record does something unusual. The government section names the king, the queen and the prince, gives their full names and their nicknames, and begins the sentence about how the family came to rule.

"They came into power by"

That is where it ends. Mid-sentence, no full stop. Whoever was writing knew the answer was going to be interesting enough to need care, and put the pen down to come back to it.

Around the walls the island is worse than the sea was. A time-warp tunnel in the Crystal Cavern lets creatures from the distant past walk into the present, and the inhabitants have grown accustomed to them, which is a remarkable thing to grow accustomed to. Fire giants raid out of magma tunnels with siege engines built from trees and rock, riding enslaved dinosaurs, led by a king called Tohlrod, and the record notes they are more vicious than their mainland relatives. The stone giants want nothing except to be left in their tunnels carving walls and worshipping, and they send emissaries to the town occasionally, looking for inspiration.

That last detail is the one that sticks. Two hundred shipwrecked sailors, and the master masons of the island come to them for ideas.

There is an open thread sitting in the middle of it. The stone giant princess has been taken by one of the villainous inhabitants of Bo'Cha. A shaman has been sent after her, has tracked her to the Crystal Cave, and has already seen her imprisonment in a vision. He is on his way.

The record leaves him walking.
