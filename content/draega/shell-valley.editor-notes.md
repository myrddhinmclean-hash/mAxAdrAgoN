## 2. Flagged Gaps & Canon Discrepancies

- **Geographic Placement & Stakes**: The source module is titled `Shell Valley: The Heartwood Descent` (identified as `Temple of TerTah` in drive metadata) and does not specify a global region or the overarching threat. Per the owner-approved frontmatter summary (*"Grimbah invites you to save the world in the far north, at the Druid's Temple in Shell Valley"*), the far north setting, the temple naming, and the world-saving stakes are established as owner canon.
- **The Primordial Rite**: The source notes that Grimbah requires witnesses and escorts for a primordial rite, but never elaborates on the mechanics, entity, or specific consequence if the rite fails. This gap is highlighted directly in the entry prose.
