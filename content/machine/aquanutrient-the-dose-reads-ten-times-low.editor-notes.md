CONTRADICTIONS FOUND, AND THIS ONE NEEDS A RULING
- The source says the dose "reads about ten times low" and in the same sentence says the program tells the user to "quarter it and titrate". Either the register's wording is loose, or the dose reads ten times HIGH and the register has it backwards, or "quarter it" refers to something other than the printed dose. I have reproduced both halves exactly as recorded rather than picking one and smoothing it, because guessing here means publishing a dosing instruction that is wrong by a factor of forty.
