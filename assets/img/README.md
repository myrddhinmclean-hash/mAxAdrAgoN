# Images for the Draega register

The 2020 Rivals and Destiny artwork was not recoverable from any drive on this
machine. These are the replacements to source. Save them here under the exact
filenames below, then uncomment the two slot lines at the top of
`assets/css/draega.css`.

## What to get

| filename | what it needs to be | where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `draega-hero.jpg` | dark plate armour or a helm, shot close, lit from one side, plenty of near-black around the centre | the band behind "The World of Draega" |
| `draega-band.jpg` | chainmail or worn leather, flat-on, filling the frame with no subject | texture behind the entries list |

Both get an 82% black gradient over them, so pick images that are **already
dark and low-contrast**. A bright or busy photograph will fight the heading
text and lose. Landscape, 1600px wide or more, saved as JPG at quality ~80.

## Where to get them, free and licensed for commercial use

Unsplash and Pexels are both free for commercial use with no attribution
required, which matters because this site carries a Products page. Do not pull
fantasy art off an image search — most of it is someone's copyrighted work, and
a storefront is exactly the wrong place to be casual about that.

- Chainmail, flat texture: <https://unsplash.com/s/photos/chainmail>
- Knight armour, close and dark: <https://unsplash.com/s/photos/knight-armor>
- Plate and helms: <https://unsplash.com/s/photos/armor>
- Wider medieval subjects: <https://unsplash.com/s/photos/medieval-knight>
- Second opinion, different library: <https://www.pexels.com/search/knight%20armor/>
- Chain and mail textures: <https://pixabay.com/images/search/chain-mail/>

Pixabay's licence has changed over the years; check the licence line on the
individual image rather than trusting the site generally.

## Character portraits

The `.cast` grid in `draega.css` takes portraits at a 3:4 ratio. An empty frame
renders as a dark silhouette rather than a broken image, so the grid is safe to
put on the page before the portraits exist.

Commissioned or AI-generated character art is fine here since it is yours.
Stock photography of real people is not - it reads wrong next to illustrated
characters, and model releases rarely cover fictional-character use.
